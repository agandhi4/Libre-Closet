# Redesign plan (2026-09-26)

Status: **proposed**, for the owner to decide the questions at the end. Tracked in #43. The backlog
stays in GitHub issues: once approved, each slice under Migration becomes an issue on the Roadmap
(#27), linking to its section here. The features themselves are designed in
[the wardrobe features plan](2026-09-26-wardrobe-features.md). This document decides how they fit
together on a phone: the model, the navigation, the screens and the look.

**Why now.** Every feature so far added controls to the upstream Libre Closet layout: a three-item
dock, a hamburger drawer that holds only the email and sign-out, pages built from stacked daisyUI
cards and buttons. The 390 px screenshots of the demo persona (CI, `screenshots-390px`) show the
cost:

- **The wardrobe shows two garments above the fold.** The heading, the Garments/Capsules tabs, the
  tagging prompt, the wardrobe switcher and the result count come first. A fixed search and filter
  bar then covers about 100 px above the dock.
- **Tiles crop the cutouts.** `object-cover` on a square crops tall garments like jeans and coats,
  and every tile carries a card body with the name and category.
- **The outfit builder is a form.** It shows category labels, arrows, drag handles and a red × per
  row. The outfit cannot be seen as an outfit.
- **The garment page leads with upload controls** ("Choose file", "Update photo"). Its actions are a
  column of outline buttons, and it never says how often the garment was worn or what it goes with.
- **The calendar** is a mini month above one tall card per day. Outfits show as 16 px chips, and "+
  Build outfit" is the only way to add one.
- **Defects found along the way:** the outfit card's calendar dropdown (`relative z-10`) draws over
  the dock (`06-demo-outfits`). The dock marks a tab active only when the URL equals its href
  exactly, so the garment page, a filtered grid and `/outfits/new` show no active tab. The shell
  slice (R2) fixes both.

**What converges:** a dock of about five places; cutouts on a plain background; composing by stacked
category rows with pin and shuffle, beside a free canvas; the calendar as the wear log with cost per
wear derived from it; a daily pick of about three weather-aware options; the garment page listing
its outfits and wear count.

**What to avoid:** docks spent on social, shop and resale tabs; unexplained suggestions with no way
to say why not; free canvases (fiddly at phone size, inconsistent scale); basics such as stats and a
dense grid treated as extras; slow, lossy entry of garments. This app already avoids most of these
by design: no social features, and the "Say why not" reasons from plan section 3.

## 1. The model

The owner's model from #43, settled. There are five nouns, and each has one home.

| Concept | What it is | Home | Seen from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Garment** | One piece, with N identical copies (`quantity`, #7) | Wardrobe | Styling rows, outfit tiles, Today |
| **Capsule** | A named subset of the closet; the implicit Closet is every unarchived garment | Wardrobe › Capsules | A **scope** chip on Wardrobe, Styling and Outfits |
| **Outfit** | A saved combination of garments (`outfit_slot`), private to its owner | Outfits › Saved | Calendar, Today, the garment page |
| **Calendar entry** | An outfit planned for a day and occasion (#13) | Calendar | Today |
| **Wear** | What was actually worn, snapshotted per garment and day (#7) | Garment page (history) | Laundry, Insights |

**Decisions**

- **A capsule is a scope, not a place.** One `?capsule=` chip means the same thing on every surface
  that lists garments or outfits: the grid, Styling's rows, the Ideas generator's pool, the Saved
  outfits. Capsules are managed in one place (the Wardrobe tab), and everywhere else they are used
  through one scope control. This is the pattern `?ownerId=` already follows for shared wardrobes.
- **An outfit is a record, not an event.** Planning and wearing happen on the calendar entry, and
  wears are snapshots (#7). So the outfit page never needs its own "worn" state. It shows its
  entries ("Planned Tue", "Worn 4 times") by reading them.
- **One way to make an outfit row, three ways to arrive at it.** Every path ends in the same write:
  the outfit and its slots, and optionally the calendar entry, in one transaction (today's
  `POST /outfits`).
  1. **Compose: Styling** (#42). The stacked per-role carousels. The builder becomes Styling:
     `builder.ts`'s cycle engine and row fragment are kept, and the page, the rows by role and the
     scroll-snap presentation are new. There is one implementation of stacked rows. Editing a saved
     outfit opens it in Styling.
  2. **Suggest: the generator** (#9, `src/web/gallery/generate.ts`, pure). It has one API, "fill
     these roles given these locked garments, in this scope, for this destination". It serves
     Styling's **Shuffle** (the unlocked rows), Outfits › **Ideas** (whole cards) and **Today's**
     suggestions (#15). No surface gets its own suggestion logic.
  3. **Choose: Outfits** (#9's gallery). Saved outfits and generated Ideas, browsed as whole outfits
     and picked for a day. "Edit" on any card hands its garments to Styling, so the gallery never
     grows a second composer.
- **The destination travels with the user.** Where a new outfit will go is one parsed value,
  `OutfitDestination` (`none`, `day:YYYY-MM-DD` with an occasion, and later `trip:ID`), carried as
  `?for=` by Styling, Outfits and the "Plan" sheet. It decides the primary action's label and effect
  ("Save", "Wear on Tue, evening"). Plan section 3 already defines `?for=` for the gallery. This
  extends it to Styling, so "+ Plan" on a calendar day can go either way and come back.
- **Wearing an outfit always goes through the calendar** (plan section 1). "Wear this" anywhere
  schedules the outfit for today and marks it worn. "Wore today" on a garment page logs one loose
  wear.

## 2. Information architecture

### The dock

Five places, labelled, phone-first. Actions are not places: there is no centre "+".

```
┌────────┬──────────┬────────┬─────────┬──────────┐
│ Today  │ Wardrobe │ Style  │ Outfits │ Calendar │
└────────┴──────────┴────────┴─────────┴──────────┘
```

- **Today** arrives with #15 (`/` becomes Today, plan section 9). Until then the dock has four items
  and `/` stays the wardrobe.
- **Style**, not "Styling", as the dock label, because it fits at 78 px and reads as a verb. The
  page's title is "Styling".
- **Outfits**, not "Gallery". It is the user's word for what they hold. "Gallery" stays the code's
  name for #9's swipe presentation.
- **No centre "+"**, unlike Acloset. The only frequent add is a garment, which belongs to the
  Wardrobe's header. Android's share sheet already opens the link import, and the manifest shortcuts
  keep "Add garment". A centre action would cost a sixth slot, or cost Today.
- **Active by prefix.** A tab is active for its whole subtree: `/wardrobe/*` and `/capsules/*` are
  Wardrobe; `/styling` is Style; `/outfits/*` is Outfits. One route table in `layout/` owns this. It
  replaces the exact-href match.

### The app bar

The hamburger drawer goes. It holds two links today. The top bar becomes the page's own header: its
title, its scope (the wardrobe switcher, the capsule chip) and at most two actions, with the
**avatar** at the right. The avatar opens **Profile** (`/auth/profile`), a page with sections:
account (email, password, delete), **Sharing** (today's `/wardrobe-share/manage`), notifications
(push, #15), weather location and temperature offset (#14), the week template (#16), the style
profile (#34), agent access for the MCP server (#33), About, and Sign out. The freshness indicator
and the request spinner stay in the bar.

## 3. Screens

Low-fi at 390 px. Each screen keeps the conventions it already follows: boosted links, htmx
fragments, scroll-snap with no JS library, and a sentinel for paging.

│ Wardrobe ▾                     ⋯   ＋  ◉ │ switcher in the title; add; avatar
│ Closet   Capsules   Laundry   Wishlist   │ underline tabs
│ ◌ Office ✕   Tops   Warm     ⌕  ⚟  64    │ sticky scope row: chips, search, filters, count
├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐   │
│ │          │ │          │ │        • │   │ 3 columns, 4:5, cutout contained
│ │  cutout  │ │  cutout  │ │  cutout  │   │ on the plinth colour; • needs a wash
│ │          │ │  ×3      │ │          │   │ ×3 copies (#7)
│ └──────────┘ └──────────┘ └──────────┘   │
│ Navy tee     Raw jeans    Chore coat     │ one line, optional (density toggle)
│ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐   │
│ …                                        │
├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Today   Wardrobe   Style   Outfits  Cal │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **The scope row replaces the fixed bottom bar.** It is sticky under the header and holds the
  capsule chip, active filter chips, search (it expands in place) and the filter sheet. It shows
  about nine garments above the fold, where today there are two.
- The switcher (shared wardrobes) is the title's menu, not a full-width select.
- Tiles have no card chrome. The cutout is shown with `object-contain` on the plinth colour, and the
  original photo with `object-cover` when there is no cutout. Marks stay small: needs a wash,
  copies, away (#7).

├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Today   Wardrobe   Style   Outfits  Cal │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **One row per role**, in outfit order: layer, top, bottom, footwear, then accessories and bags as
  one row of smaller items. Each row is a scroll-snap strip with its neighbours peeking. Empty roles
  are hidden. The **Rows** toggles are Whering's 2/3/4-piece control in this app's terms. Choosing
  Dress swaps top and bottom for the one-piece row.
- **Lock and Shuffle.** Lock pins a row, and Shuffle asks the generator for the unlocked rows. "No
  garment" is a stop in each strip, the builder's position 0.
- **Entry points:** the dock (a fresh stack); "Style this" on a garment (`?with=`, that row locked
  on it); a capsule; "Edit" on an outfit (`?outfit=`); "+ Plan" on a calendar day (`?for=`). Save
  opens a small sheet: name (generated, editable), and schedule or not when there is no `?for=`.
- A shared wardrobe (VIEW) can browse and shuffle but never save (#42).

## 4. Visual direction

**What holds in every option:**

- **Imagery.** Cutouts on one neutral **plinth** colour (`base-200`) everywhere a garment appears,
  contained, never cropped. Pages use no other imagery: the garments are the colour.
- **Theme.** A custom daisyUI 5 theme (`@plugin "daisyui/theme"` in `views/assets/main.css`),
  semantic tokens only in markup (`bg-base-200`, `text-primary`), never raw palette classes. The
  chip hues (`.chip-0..5`) are replaced by the occasion, which carries meaning. The manifest's
  `theme_color` and `background_color` (hard-coded in `src/web/shell/manifest.ts`) and a
  `<meta name="theme-color">` per scheme come from the same values.
- **Dark mode** follows the system (`--prefersdark`), with a dark theme of the same design. The
  plinth stays lighter than the page in dark, so dark garments remain visible. CI shoots the key
  pages in both schemes.
- **Type.** Self-hosted from `node_modules` (`@fontsource-variable/*`, a Latin subset, precached by
  the worker). No CDN, per the Stack. A variable font costs about 30 to 50 KB.
- **Motion.** Native scroll-snap and press states only (`:active`, the in-flight dimming that
  exists). No htmx view transitions: they block taps for about 250 ms (layout.tsx's config). Sheets
  slide up in 150 ms. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Density.** A 3-column grid, 44 px touch targets, 16 px gutters, no shadows on tiles.

**The options** (tokens abbreviated; each is two daisyUI themes, light and dark):

|  | **A. Atelier** (recommended) | **B. Gallery** | **C. Soft utility** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feel | Warm paper and ink; a tailor's notebook | Editorial white; a shop's lookbook | Friendly and rounded; today's app, refined |
| Type | **Fraunces** (headings, optical size) + **Inter** (UI) | **Inter Tight** only; small caps labels, tracked | **Manrope** only |
| `base-100 / 200 / content` | `oklch(98% .006 85)` / `oklch(94% .01 80)` / `oklch(24% .01 60)` | `oklch(100% 0 0)` / `oklch(96% 0 0)` / `oklch(15% 0 0)` | `oklch(98% .005 250)` / `oklch(95% .01 250)` / `oklch(27% .02 260)` |
| `primary` | ink `oklch(24% .01 60)` | black `oklch(15% 0 0)` | sage `oklch(55% .07 150)` |
| `accent` | clay `oklch(62% .12 45)`, used sparingly (lock, worn) | none: state shown by weight and underline | apricot `oklch(78% .11 70)` |
| Radius (`box`/`field`/`selector`) | `.75rem` / `.5rem` / `1rem` | `0` / `0` / `0` | `1.25rem` / `.75rem` / `2rem` |
| `--depth` / `--border` | `0` / `1px` hairlines | `0` / `1px` | `1` / `1px` |
| Dark | warm charcoal `oklch(20% .008 60)`, plinth `oklch(30% .01 60)` | true black `oklch(0% 0 0)` (OLED), plinth `oklch(22% 0 0)` | slate `oklch(22% .02 260)` |
| Risk | The serif must stay in headings only | Austere; state that relies on weight alone can be missed | Closest to the upstream look; the least identity |

**Recommendation: A.** The neutrals leave the garments as the only colour, which is the point of a
wardrobe app. The serif headings give the app an identity of its own without branding tricks. It is

(so the CI artifact compares before and after), and adds shots only for new screens. Pages not yet
redesigned keep working under the new theme and shell.
