# Plan item candidates (34b)

Moved whole from `src/web/plans/CLAUDE.md` (#312) to keep that file under its cap; the short form there carries the rules.

- **Candidates (34b)**: a plan item's candidate products are **wishlist garments**, linked in `plan_item_candidate` (`(plan_item_id, garment_id)` primary key, both cascade, index on `garment_id`; `drizzle/0017_plan_item_candidate.sql`). A link, not a `plan_item_id` column on the garment: one product can stand for the same item in two plans (a duplicate copies the links), and the wishlist is shared with MANAGE grantees while plans are not, so no garment row a grantee reads or edits carries plan data. **One writer**, `changeCandidates` (`candidates.ts`, a Queryable, both sides locked `FOR SHARE`, `changeMembership`'s shape): only items of the owner's plans and garments of their wardrobe, and only wishlist garments are added. **At most `MAX_CANDIDATES_PER_ITEM` (5) per item**, a curated few to choose between (and what bounds a candidate's cost on the shopping list, #18b): an add past it throws `TooManyCandidates` (a 400 `HttpError`) before anything is written, counted under `lockOwner` taken first in the transaction, so two adds at once cannot both pass (`candidate-cap.spec.ts` holds one open while the other waits). Only candidates still on the wishlist count (a bought one frees its place), and only an item that gains one is judged, so an item already past the cap keeps its links and may drop some. The pickers re-render their form 400 with the reason and what was ticked; the garment form's and the link import's `planItem` are refused ahead by `requireCandidateRoom` (in `resolveCandidateFor`), and the writer's throw rolls back a garment saved with the link; `add_candidate` answers the error (by url before the page is fetched). Readers (`candidatesOfPlan`, `candidatesOfItems`, `candidaciesOf`) go through `onWishlist`, so a bought candidate's link stops mattering on its own. Added from the item (`GET|POST .../items/:itemId/candidates`: a link or a photo through the wishlist's own add carrying `planItem`, or ticking what is on the wishlist; the form posts `shown`, the capsule picker's rule) and from the wishlist item (`GET|POST /wardrobe/:id/plan-items`). `planItem` on the new garment form and the link import (`DestinationQuery`, `GarmentBody`) is resolved by `resolveCandidateFor` (the requester's own item in their own wardrobe, else a 404 before anything is stored) and linked in the garment's own transaction (`WithGarment`, `createGarment`/`createGarmentWithPendingPhoto`) by `linkNewCandidate`, which throws the item's 404 when the link was not made (the item deleted during a slow page fetch), so the garment rolls back instead of staying on the wishlist unlinked (#123; `add_candidate` by url too). **A candidate link is not a proposal**: it changes nothing the plan asks for and counts for nothing (matching reads the closet), so `add_candidate` writes it as the owner's form would.
