# The plan page's views (#312)

`GET /wardrobe/plans/:id[?view=items|outfits]` (`src/web/plans/plan-page.tsx`).

- **`?view=`** is URL state: `planView()` falls back to Items for anything else (never a 400). Both panels are in the page and the tabs (`PlanViewTabs`, daisyUI radio tabs) switch them locally with CSS (`PLAN_VIEW_PANELS`, `:has(:checked)`), so a switch works offline: `?view=outfits` is its own service-worker cache key, and a link per tab fell to the offline page. `?view=` only picks the initially checked tab (`planViewUrl` is still what redirects and back links use). Items shows the sections and the declined list; Outfits shows the look cards (#314, below). Tab counts include declined items and looks.
- **Items by type**: sections by role, top to toe (`planSections`), each heading with its count, in a grid of 2 columns at base, 3 at `sm`, 4 at `lg`, 5 at `xl` (CSS only, one markup). A card is a button: the photo (the owned garment's, else the top candidate's, else the glyph), the name and **one status line** (`statusLine`: "Owned", "To buy · $90" from the top-ranked candidate, "2 of 3", "To review", "With your agent"). It opens the item's `<dialog>` sheet (`ItemSheet`, native `showModal`), which holds what the card used to: status chip, budget, priority, options and Add a product, In your closet, the reason, the owner's note, the review moves, Change this, Edit item; every action is the existing route.
- **Header**: one tally line (`PlanTally`); a draft with only proposals has no "0 owned" tally, the proposals banner (with Review) says what waits (an active plan with no accepted items but proposals keeps just its Active badge). Shopping list and Compare are in the plan's ⋯ menu.
- **The plans list card** counts a draft with nothing accepted as "N proposed · N looks", never "0 items".
- The page's statement count is pinned in `plan-look-reactions.spec.ts` (6); views add none.
- **Outfits as look cards** (#314; `look-cards.tsx`, `PlanLookCards`): `groupLooks()`'s loved then proposed looks in a grid (`LOOK_GRID`, `layout/columns.ts`: 1 column on a phone, 2 at `sm`, 3 at `xl`), the revise and declined looks in collapsed `<details>` (`#plan-looks-revise`, `#plan-looks-declined`) below, and `#plan-no-looks` (ask the agent) with none. A card (`li#look-N[data-look]`): name, reaction chip, occasion line, the agent's note and the owner's, **every slot as its own tile** (2-column grid, slot order: the photo big on the plinth colour, its name, a To buy badge; a missing slot is a dashed place), and its moves at the foot (`mt-auto`, so a row's cards line up): Save as outfit, Love it, Not for me, Change this…, Reconsider, each the existing native post. No strip, no script and no `data-strip-action` (`LookSaveAction plain`). The Outfits tab's From-your-plan row keeps its strip. Every action is 44 px tall on a coarse pointer (`min-h-11`; `fine:btn-sm` under a mouse). A loved look leads with Save as outfit; one that is loved but still missing a piece cannot be saved, so **Change this…** is its primary (the missing slots carry no link to a shopping candidate, so there is no "shop it" target without a new query). Statement count unchanged (6, flat in the number of looks; `plan-look-reactions.spec.ts` compares 1 look with 8).
