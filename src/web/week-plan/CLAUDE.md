# Weekly auto-plan

`src/wardrobe/week.ts` and `src/wardrobe/week-planner.ts` (pure), `src/web/week-plan/`, `src/web/mcp/tools/week-plan.ts` (#16, plan section 12). "Plan my week" fills the week template's empty slots with the generator's outfits, and a daily re-plan swaps its own picks when the forecast changes or an outfit can no longer be worn. **The signed-in user's own, like the calendar**: no route takes `?ownerId=`, another's batch is a 404.

- **The week template is the one model of the week** (owner direction on #16: no second, disagreeing model). `week_template` (user, `weekday` 0 = Sunday to 6, `occasion`; `drizzle/0022_week_plan.sql`): per weekday at most one of `DAY_OCCASIONS` (the day's outfit: all day, work, daytime; the partial unique index `week_template_user_id_weekday_day_unique`) and any of the others around it (workout, evening, night out). #34a's `style_rhythm` ("work 3 a week", "evening 3 a month") is gone: counts cannot say which days to plan, and two stored models would disagree the first time one is edited. The rhythm is **derived** (`weeklyRhythm`: on how many weekdays each occasion comes round), shown read-only on the style page (linking to Profile › Your week) and answered by `get_style_profile` beside the template. The migration spread each weekly count onto weekdays (work from Monday, all day and daytime from the weekend back, the others spread Monday, Thursday, Saturday...) and dropped the monthly ones, which no weekday holds (only the seed's demo had rows; `week-plan-migration.spec.ts`). Occasional evenings are planned by hand. One writer, `saveWeekTemplate` (replaced whole); one form, `WeekTemplateBody`/`readWeekTemplateForm` (`template.ts`), which the seed posts through too. The editor is the Profile's `#week` section (`WeekTemplateSettings`, `views.tsx`): per weekday a select for the day's outfit and chips for the rest, **one Save** (a native `PostForm`, 303 to `/auth/profile?weekSaved=1#week`), not save-on-change: seven rows are one decision, and a half-edited week must not be planned from.
- **The planner** (`planWeek`, pure, `week-planner.spec.ts`): the template's slots of the days asked (today and the six after it), in day and occasion order; a slot is **empty** when no entry has its occasion that day, and a day occasion's slot when no day occasion is planned (one outfit dresses the day); today's slots whose window (`OCCASION_HINTS`) has ended are left. Each slot's idea is `generateIdeas` over the planner's pool with that slot's `weatherNeeds` (the occasion's window of that day's forecast, the personal offset) and the occasion's formality, seeded by (day, occasion) (`slotSeed`, `PLANNER_VERSION`); the best idea is taken, a near miss included (its problems are reported); a slot with none stays empty (`unfilled`). **Wash limits across the week**: the pool is every closet garment not away, dirty ones too (`weekPool`, beside `ideaPool`), with its wash state today; the unworn entries in the window and every outfit planned so far add their wear **days**, and a garment is offered only while `cleanCopies` stays above zero after all of them, so a planned wear never dirties a copy a later planned day relies on (a garment already worn that day costs nothing more). Nothing is washed in between. **No outfit twice**: each planned outfit joins the saved outfits the generator never repeats. Rotation: a garment the week already wears counts as just worn for later slots.
- **Writes** (`plan.ts`, `planMyWeek`): the forecast is read first (`weekForecast`, outside any lock), then one transaction under `lockOwner`: the template, the window's entries (`windowEntries`), the pool, saved outfits and avoided pairs are read **after** the lock, so a double tap or a retried `plan_week` waits and finds its slots filled (`/calendar?planned=none`). Each outfit goes through **`pickIdea`** (the gallery's pick: reused or created and named `ideaName`) with `plannedBy: 'auto'`, its entry recorded in the batch. Tables: `week_plan` (the batch: one tap, for the banner and Undo) and `week_plan_entry` (entry, batch, whether the planner created the outfit, and the targets it was planned for: `torso`, `limbs`, `layer`, `rain`, all null without a forecast).
- **`outfit_calendar.planned_by`** (`user` | `auto`, default and every entry before #16 `user`): who owns the choice. **Every writer that edits an entry makes it the person's**: `setEntryWorn` (marking or unmarking), `updateOutfit` (the calendar chip's edit link is the outfit form; every auto entry of the outfit), `setEntryOutfit` (Change, #69, which also removes the planner's outfit it replaced once unheld, through Undo's `removeUnheldOutfits`) and `insertEntry` when the person plans the outfit already auto on that day (the outfit page's Plan, the plan page's and the Saved tab's pick, `schedule_outfit`, a pick with that day: #77, see Calendar). **A person's pick of a planner-made outfit makes the outfit theirs** (`adoptPlannerOutfit`, see Outfit gallery): its entries stay the planner's, but Undo, a swap and Change no longer delete it (`outfit_created` is what `removeAutoEntries` passes to `removeUnheldOutfits`; `outfitIsHeld` is not widened: it answers "what references this outfit", a different question). Only `auto` entries are ever re-planned or undone. The row carries an Auto badge (`OccasionRow`, `data-auto`), `get_calendar` its `plannedBy`.
- **Undo** (`undoWeekPlan`, under `lockOwner`): the batch's entries still `auto` are deleted with each outfit the planner created for them that nothing holds any more (`removeAutoEntries` and `removeUnheldOutfits`: no calendar entry and no trip, `outfitIsHeld`; through `deleteOutfit`); entries taken over since stay; the batch goes once empty.
- **The daily re-plan** (`replan.ts`): `replanToday(deps, userId, now)` is one user's re-plan for the day, **once, whoever asks**: the minutely run (`replanWeeks(deps, now)`, every user `replanCandidates` lists: an auto entry recorded in a batch from today on and no claim today) and **the morning reminder, which re-plans its person first** (#76, see Web Push). Scheduled by `server.ts` only (every minute through `scheduleMinutely`, with or without `WEATHER_ENABLED`: without a forecast it still judges availability; claims pruned nightly), never by `createApp()`, so the harness and the CLIs never re-plan (a spec calls it with the instant it wants). The run does nothing before **`REPLAN_HOUR`, derived from `REMINDER_WINDOWS.morning.from`** (05:00 on the household's wall clock, `hourIn`): everyone is re-planned by the earliest time a reminder can be set to, and a swap notice comes before a reminder; the reminder does not depend on it. **The claim commits with the work**: per user one transaction under `lockOwner` that reads the auto entries, claims the day (`claimReplan`: an insert into `week_replan (user_id, day)`, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING`; none without an auto entry, so a week planned later today is judged by a later run), judges and writes. A second caller (the next minute, the reminder, a second server in an overlapping deploy) waits on the owner lock and finds the day claimed and the re-plan committed, never half done, which is what lets a reminder read the day after it. A re-plan that throws is logged and the day claimed on its own afterwards (`claimAfterFailure`), so it is tried once a day, not every minute; one that timed out waiting on the owner lock (`OwnerLockTimeout`, see Calendar) judged nothing and is logged `deferred`, the day left unclaimed for the next minute; the swap notice goes after the commit (a crash between loses the notice, never doubles it). The judgement (`replanWeek`, pure): **an outfit that can no longer be worn** (`unwearableOn`, `isAvailable` on the planned day's terms: a garment archived or a wishlist item, lent or at repair, deleted since (an empty slot, only telling in an outfit the planner created: every slot had a garment; a reused saved outfit may have one empty on purpose), or, **on today only**, without a clean copy and not worn today) is swapped for the slot's best idea under the planner's rules, whatever the forecast, and kept (logged) when nothing else dresses the slot. Dirty counts only on the day itself because today's wash state is a fact and a later day's a projection the next load of laundry changes; the run is daily, so a garment still dirty on its day is caught that morning, before the person dresses (the planner's own "nothing is washed in between" is right for filling a week and too eager for undoing one). Otherwise the weather: an entry whose targets (torso, limbs, layer, rain) did not change or whose day has no forecast is kept; with changed targets an outfit that still fits them is kept (recording the new targets), else the slot is planned again and **swapped only for an idea that answers the weather better** (the old entry and its planner-made outfit removed, the new one in the same batch). What swapped is **pushed** to the person's devices **with the morning reminder on**, one line per swap saying why ("Thursday turned cold: swapped in Wool coat", "Bean Boots is at repair: swapped in Chelsea boots on Thursday", "A garment of Friday's outfit was deleted: swapped in ..."; title "Your week changed", opens `/calendar`, tag `week-replan`, TTL 6 h): the notice is about what to wear, the reminder's subject, so no new setting; without `PWA_ENABLED` swaps happen and nobody is told. **One push per swap, never two**: when the morning reminder runs the re-plan (`replanToday(..., { announcesToday: true })`), today's swaps are said in the reminder itself and left out of the notice, which then goes only for other days' swaps (if any); the minutely run announces everything, today included. `replanToday` returns every swap it made either way. `outfitGarmentStates` (`queries.ts`) reads the auto entries' outfits' slots with each garment's status, away, wash state (`wearsSinceWashSql`) and whether it was worn today, in one statement.
- **Routes** (`routes.tsx`): `POST /calendar/plan-week` (no input; no template: 303 to `/auth/profile#week`; else 303 to `/calendar?planned=<batch>|none`), `POST /calendar/plan-week/:id/undo` (303 to `/calendar?undone=N`; another's: 404), `POST /auth/profile/week` (`WeekTemplateBody`; an occasion outside its field's set is a 400). The calendar reads `?planned=` (the banner: the batch's entries, the template slots still empty, Undo) and `?undone=` (a toast) as navigation state, both stripped from the address (`StripFlags`); the "Plan my week" button is static, so `/calendar` stays byte-stable. Today shows the same button.
- **Logs**: `Week planned for user <id> from <day> (plan <id>): N outfit(s) (entries ...), M slot(s) unfilled, forecast for D day(s) in T ms` (context `Web`; `(MCP)` from the tool), the undo and the template save; context `WeekPlan`: `Week re-plan runs every minute`, per user `Week re-plan for user <id> on <day>: N swapped (<day> <occasion> <change>|<reason> garment <id>|deleted, ...), K kept in T ms`, an entry that cannot be worn and has no replacement (`... cannot be worn (<reason> garment <id>) and nothing else dresses its slot; kept`), a failure (error, the day claimed), one line per run that re-planned someone, the prune.
- **Seed**: Theo's week table is his template (each day's `Calendar` occasion, a workout where the day has one), and his planned week is "Plan my week"'s over the seven days after the anchor, with the stand-in's forecast (demo.md step 7); the simulation plans only the evenings there.

## Gotchas

- **An auto entry is the planner's only until someone touches it.** `outfit_calendar.planned_by` is what keeps the daily re-plan and Undo off a person's choice (#16): a new writer that edits an entry (its outfit, its occasion, worn) must set it to `user` in the same transaction, as `setEntryWorn`, `updateOutfit` and `setEntryOutfit` do, or the next morning's re-plan may swap out what they chose. **Choosing what the planner chose is touching it**: `insertEntry` adopts an `auto` entry the person plans again (`ON CONFLICT ... DO UPDATE ... WHERE`), and `pickIdea` adopts a planner-made outfit the person picks (`adoptPlannerOutfit`); a new path where the person picks or plans something that may already be the planner's goes through them, never around (an `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` of its own left #77's entries `auto`). Only the planner writes `auto` (through `pickIdea`'s `plannedBy`), and a new reader of "the planner's entries" filters `planned_by = 'auto'` rather than joining `week_plan_entry` alone (that row stays, as provenance, after the person takes the entry over).
