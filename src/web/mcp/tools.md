# MCP tools: each tool's contract

The per-tool reference for `src/web/mcp/tools/`; the rules every tool keeps are in `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Tools** (`tools/`): read `get_wardrobe` (#277: the whole closet in one compact answer, no paging: `garmentSummaries` with no page limit over `inCloset`, or `owned` with `includeArchived`; id, name, category, type, colours, materials, warmth, formality, brand, condition, copies, never photos or links; the owner's `wears` and `lastWorn` from `wearCountsByGarment`, one grouped read, absent for a grantee as in `get_garment`), `search_garments` (the grid's filters through `garmentSummaries`, which shares `gridWhere`; a type needs its category; `needsWash` on a shared wardrobe is a 403, where the grid silently drops it; `needsTagging`, #90, is tagging mode's queue through the same `needsTags` predicate as `GridFilters.needsTags`, closet garments only whatever `includeArchived` says), `get_garment` (no photo; its status; its `careLabel`; the owner's wears and washes, `cost` and `costPerWear` (`totalCost`, repairs included, #151), and the repair log, only on their own: a grantee gets the price alone), `get_garment_photo` (#90, below), `list_wishlist` (`wishlistItems`: price, link, what each replaces; search_garments never lists the wishlist), `list_capsules`, `get_capsule`, `list_outfits`, `get_outfit`, `get_calendar` (up to 62 days, the household's days; each entry's `selfie: true|false`, never the image, #19), `laundry_status`, `wardrobe_stats` (`tools/insights.ts`: the insights page's figures through the same `readInsights`, the caller's own, `unwornDays` 30, 90 or 180; each cost entry carries `repairCost` beside `price` and `cost`), `list_shared_wardrobes` (the first name, never an email), `compare_with_shared_wardrobe` (`compare.ts`, pure: the closet vs a shared one by role and type, gaps with brands, colours, prices and links); write, each description starting `WRITES`: `add_garment_from_link` (`importLink` + `importedForm`, the caller's overrides, then the form's own post through `formPost` and `readGarmentForm` and `createGarmentWithPendingPhoto`; the first readable photo, no choice; a refused form discards the pending photo; `destination` wishlist by default (a product link is usually being considered; `closet` for something owned) and an optional `replacesGarmentId`; saved to the closet, its answer's `lookalikes` are the garment form's duplicate check after the fact, `closetLookalikes`, #20; `planItemId`, `candidateNote` and `candidateRank` are retired with plans, #337: given, the call is a 410 before any fetch), `add_garment_copy` (`addCopies`, the web's "Add a copy": more copies of a closet garment, owner or MANAGE, 409 past `QUANTITY_MAX` or off the closet), `update_garment` (properties and the care label through `storedPropertyValues`, `withPresets` for a new type, `withCarePresets` for new materials, `readProperties` and `readCareLabel`; the role's missing properties named as `ignored`; condition through `readCondition`/`setCondition`), `set_capsule_membership` (`changeMembership`), `create_outfit` (slots are the garments' categories; another's garment is refused, not stored empty), `schedule_outfit` and `create_outfit`'s plan (an `occasion`, all day when omitted; several outfits a day, each outfit once a day, which keeps the occasion it has and returns it; #13; `schedule_outfit`'s optional `replaceEntryId` changes that entry's outfit instead, `replaceEntryOutfit` with the page's rules, #69), `get_calendar` (each entry's occasion, a day's entries in occasion order), `mark_worn` (an entry through `setEntryWorn`, or a garment's Wore today), `mark_washed`; with `WEATHER_ENABLED` (`mcpTools`), read `get_weather` (the user's location, days from today within the 16-day forecast, and per day what it asks of an occasion's outfit: `dayWeather` over the matching), and `get_calendar` adds the same to each entry within the forecast. The style profile (`tools/style.ts`): read `get_style_profile`. **Muse's (#337, `tools/suggestions.ts`, `tools/coverage.ts`; the design `docs/plans/2026-10-05-muse-suggestions.md` section 5, the model `src/web/wishlist/suggestions.md`), the caller's own, no `ownerId`**: read `get_closet_coverage` (targets the agent states, category to priority, judged by `matchPlan` over `closetPieces` as a plan's gap view was: each owned, partly or missing, what fulfils it, its replace_soon matches, why; a type of another category or a range upside down is a 400; derived, never stored), `list_suggestions` (the inbox's own read, `readInbox`, every page: open needs with their options and each one's unlocks, Ready to buy, still looking, set aside with reasons and notes (the owner's own items left out), and `museOutfitsSql`'s outfits by reaction), `get_suggestion_feedback` (`readSuggestionFeedback`, `src/web/wishlist/feedback.ts`, a pure read: since the token's cursor, `personal_access_token.feedback_read_at`, every need decided, pick set aside, purchase (`garment.bought_at`, with the price paid, `different` for "a different one"), outfit reaction, and every bought suggestion's wears; one statement, writing nothing; its `until`, the read's time less `FEEDBACK_MARGIN_MS`, is what `finish_round` takes to move the cursor, `suggestions.md`; a null time is told before the first round's end or with `all` only); write `create_option_group` (`createOptionGroup`: a name open, or chosen and not bought, is a 409 naming it, one set aside a 409 with its reason), `suggest_garment` (refused before the fetch by `suggestionRoom`, one statement: a need not the caller's 404, decided or full 409, a link (`productUrlKey`) of a suggestion, or of any wishlist garment set aside, 409; then `addGarmentFromLink` on the wishlist with `markSuggestedProduct` in the garment's transaction, both rules judged again under the owner lock, a refusal rolling the garment back and discarding its photo (`mcp-muse-races.spec.ts`); `price` and `size` override the page), `finish_round` (`finishRound`, `src/web/wishlist/rounds.ts`: under the owner lock, a `muse_round` row only when something was proposed or suggested since the owner's last round, which Today's card counts live; the optional `feedbackUntil` moves the token's cursor, `moveFeedbackCursor`, round or not; then one push, `muse-round`, to the devices taking Muse's rounds; an empty round answers `round: null`, no error), `suggest_outfit` (`proposeOutfit`, the outfit writer's proposal: a garment set already the owner's or declined is a 409, one proposed and not declined answers it with `alreadyProposed`; `slotMayNameSql` as any save). Nothing of theirs buys, marks owned, archives or deletes. **Retired** (`tools/retired.ts`, until plans go in #337's part B): the plans' `create_plan`, `propose_plan_item`, `update_plan_item`, `list_plans`, `get_plan_gaps`, `get_plan_feedback`, `get_shopping_list`, `add_candidate`, `update_candidate`, `compare_plans`, `list_looks`, `propose_look` and `update_look` keep their names, take any input and answer a 410 naming the tool that replaced them. The outfit gallery's (#9; see Outfit gallery): read `suggest_outfits` (`ideasFor`, as the Ideas tab: for a day and occasion, today all day by default, optionally with a garment or from a capsule; seeded and paged; each idea with its fit to the forecast and the formality, its problems and what it rotates back in), write `pick_outfit` (an idea saved through `pickIdea`, named for its garments unless named, optionally planned; idempotent: a retry answers the same outfit with `alreadySaved: true`). Today's (#15, `tools/today.ts`): read `get_today` (`todayFor`, the home screen as data: the day, whether anything is worn, the planned rows with entry and outfit ids, the all-day suggestions while nothing dresses the day, and with the weather what today asks of an all-day outfit). Nothing deletes or archives. Read `get_sizes` (#24: the caller's measurements and brand notes, or one brand's; see Sizes). Read `goes_with_closet` (#18b: a wishlist item of the caller's by `garmentId`, as its page: the count and cap, the best few, the roles it pairs with, near-duplicates with `replacesIt`; another's item or a closet garment is "Not on your wishlist"). The weekly auto-plan's (#16, `tools/week-plan.ts`): write `plan_week` (`planMyWeek`, as the calendar's button: what it planned with entry and outfit ids and garments, the slots nothing could fill, the batch; idempotent), and `get_style_profile` answers the week template and its derived rhythm. Trips (#10, `tools/trips.ts`, the caller's own): read `list_trips`, `get_trip` (`tripModel`: days, outfits, the packing list with each garment's wears, copies needed, copies to pack, packed and warnings, the extras, and with `WEATHER_ENABLED` the destination's forecast through `tripForecast`, and past it `typicalDays`, each `typical: true` with the normals and what a typical day asks); write `plan_trip_outfit` (a saved `outfitId` through `addTripOutfit`, or an idea's `garmentIds` through `pickForTrip`; a day of the trip and an occasion optional; idempotent).
