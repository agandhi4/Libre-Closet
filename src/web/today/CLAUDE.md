# Today

`src/web/today/` (#15; plan section 9, redesign "Today"): `GET /`, the home screen, the dock's first tab and the manifest's `start_url` (its `id` stays `/wardrobe`: the installed app's identity). The signed-in user's own day, like the calendar; signed out it is the login redirect like any page. **Today stores nothing of its own**: it reads the calendar and the generator and writes through their writers.

- **The model is `todayFor`** (`today.ts`), shared by the page, the MCP tool `get_today` and the morning push reminder (which hands it the batch's weather, `ownWeather`, #173), so the three never disagree: a row per occasion planned today (`entriesSql`, shared with the calendar: occasion order, each entry with its outfit's collage garments), and, **while nothing that dresses the day is planned** (`DAY_OCCASIONS` in `src/wardrobe/occasions.ts`: all day, work, daytime), an all-day row of suggestions first: `todayIdeas`, `ideasFor` for today's forecast with the day's seed (`dailySeed`, as the gallery opens), a page of `TODAY_IDEAS` (3), and the person's weather (`weatherWithForecastSql` then `userWeatherFrom`, null with the weather off), which the line and the ideas share. A day with only a run and a dinner planned still gets ideas for what to wear between; a work day does not. Worn today (`somethingWornSql`: a worn entry or a garment's "Wore today") is read only when asked (`todayFor`'s `worn`, in the same statement), since the page never shows it: `get_today` asks, and the evening reminder reads it with the day's bare entries (`entriesOfDaySql`) for every evening person in one statement (`eveningDays`, #173): the evening names the plan and needs neither weather nor ideas, so it never builds the model.
- **The page** (`today-page.tsx`): the day's date, the weather line (`UserWeatherLine`, rendered in the page: see Reads), a card per plan an agent's token drafted while it holds proposals ("Muse drafted Spring capsule: 3 ideas to review", Review to its review page; #295, the home screen's way to the plans; the count is proposed items plus proposed looks, #291, the rule `awaitingReview` in `src/web/plans/gaps.ts` that the plan page and list share; gone once nothing is proposed, so nothing to dismiss; **then, in its place, the next step** (#302): while no draft waits, one card "<product> completes N of your loved looks" with a link to the shopping list at that product's card (`#candidate-<id>`), `rankedPurchasesSql` ranks and `nextPurchaseOf` picks the first that `itemsToBuy` still lists (`src/web/plans/candidates.ts`; skipped while a draft waits: the active plan's candidate that COMPLETES the most loved looks (`completedLookCountSql`: it is the only slot of a loved, non-declined look not an owned closet garment), ties by the strips' `lookCountSql` total then lowest garment id; none when it completes no loved look; an unnamed product reads as its `categoryLabel`; `rankedPurchasesSql` rides the day's statement as `nextPurchase`; `nextPurchaseOf` adds statements only when a product is ranked and no draft waits, see Reads), never beside a draft's card), then the rows. A planned entry is a card (`OutfitCollage`, the name) with **"Wore it"** (a `PostForm` to the calendar's `POST /calendar/:id/worn` with `worn=1&returnTo=/`: one worn write wherever it is tapped) and **"Change"** (the gallery with `?for=day:<today>&occasion=&replace=<entry>`: the pick takes the entry's place, `replaceEntryOutfit`, #69; not offered once worn); worn, it says "Worn today" with an undo (`worn=0`). An ideas row is a scroll-snap strip of cards (collage, name, `Reasons`, **"Wear this"**), **Refresh** (`GET /today/ideas?occasion=&page=`, the next page swapped over the row, `hx-target="closest [data-today-row]"`; after the last page, the first again; lenient: malformed is all day, page 1) and "More ideas" (the gallery for today). "+ Plan another outfit" opens the plan page for today.
- **"Wear this" is one transaction, and happens once** (`wearIdea`, `queries.ts`; `POST /today/wear`: `garmentId` × n and the `occasion`, a native `PostForm`, 303 back to `/`): `pickIdea` (under `lockOwner`: the outfit of exactly these garments, reused or created), then `wearOutfitOn` (planned today for the occasion through `insertEntry`, the entry marked worn through `setEntryWorn`), in the same transaction. A double tap's second transaction waits on the owner lock, finds the outfit and the entry, and `setEntryWorn` finds it worn (`changed: false`, logged "already worn"); `today-home.spec.ts` holds one transaction open while a second waits. Garments not in the owner's closet are a 404 with nothing written; a malformed post a 400.
- **Reads** (#158; production pays a ~114 ms round trip per statement, so count statements, not milliseconds; `today-home.spec.ts` and `weather.spec.ts` assert each count): **`GET /`** is the session, then the day's entries with the settings and forecast row and the waiting drafts in one statement (`readDay`: `entriesSql`, `weatherWithForecastSql` and, asked by the page only, `waitingDraftsSql` through `selectScalars`, #172, #295; two in parallel before), then, while the day is undressed, `ideasFor`'s one statement (the pool, saved outfits and clashes, #168): 3 with the weather on or off, 2 dressed. Those totals hold unless a loved-look product is ranked and no draft waits: `nextPurchaseOf` → `itemsToBuy` then adds two (the plan's items and the closet, in parallel; `plan-covers-next-step.spec.ts` asserts +2, and none with nothing loved or a draft waiting). A forecast that must be fetched (a location's first ask, a stale row) adds the cache's own statements. The line is rendered in the page from that weather read, not loaded after it as a `WeatherSlot` (the stale-while-revalidate pages must stay byte-stable; Today, network first, need not): one request and three statements fewer per open. The price: on a dressed day the page waits on a cold forecast miss (a location's first ask) that only the fragment waited on before. **Refresh** is the session, then the pool and the memory with the weather row in one statement (2). **"Wear this"** takes the owner lock once (`wearIdea` is the `ownerTransaction`; `pickIdea`, `insertEntry` and `setEntryWorn` join it, see Calendar's Owner lock): 9 on a repeat tap (the garments and the outfit they already are in one, #168), 25 before. **"Wore it"** is the calendar's `setEntryWorn` (6: the lock and its timeout are one statement, the worn mark and its wears another, #165).
- **Network first, not a stale-while-revalidate tab root** (decided in #15; `OPENS_STALE`, `src/web/page-cache.ts`): all of Today is the day's own data (the plan, the ideas, what was worn), so a byte-stable page would be a shell with a date and every row a fragment, and a copy opened first would show yesterday's plan with live "Wear this" buttons. NetworkFirst with the navigation preload answers a cold open in one round trip; offline the worker's last copy shows with its age (freshness.js) and its writes disabled (`data-needs-network`, a `data-offline-note`). `SECTION_HOME.today` is `/` (the dock's link) and is deliberately absent from the stale tab roots.
- **The dock** (`DOCK_TABS`, `src/web/layout/sections.ts`): Today · Wardrobe · Style · Outfits · Calendar, the owner's decision on #43 (2026-09-27; no centre "+"). `/` stays Today.
- **Logs** (context `Web`): `Idea worn by user <id> on <day> (<occasion>): [existing ]outfit <id> of garments <ids>, entry <id> marked worn (<n> wears logged)|already worn`; the page's model at debug.
