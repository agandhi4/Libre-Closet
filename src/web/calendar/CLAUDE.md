# Calendar

`src/web/calendar/` (ported to plain Fastify and Drizzle 2026-09-26). A calendar day is a plain date: `outfit_calendar.day` is a Postgres `date`, a `'YYYY-MM-DD'` string in TypeScript from the row through the URL and back, and all arithmetic is on day numbers in `calendar-date.ts`. No JS `Date` represents a calendar day: stepping an instant by 24 h or with local-time setters mislabels days as soon as a zone observes DST. The one conversion from an instant is `todayIn(APP_TIMEZONE, now)`: "today" (the highlight, the default week, the default month) is the household's date in `APP_TIMEZONE` (IANA name, default `America/New_York`, checked at boot), never UTC and never the container's zone. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday. `calendar-view.ts` builds the page model from plain dates (pure, unit-tested in New York time).

- **Routes**: `GET /calendar[?week=YYYY-MM-DD]` (a missing or malformed value is the current week: navigation state, never a 400; the pre-R6 mini month's `?calMonth=` is ignored); `GET /calendar/month[?month=YYYY-MM]` (the month of collages, below; owner only; malformed is this month; network first, not a tab root); `GET /calendar/plan?for=day:D&occasion=O[&replace=E]` (the plan page, below; a missing or malformed destination is today, all day; a `replace` that is not the user's entry there plans one more); `POST /calendar` (`date`, `outfitId`, optional `occasion` (all day without one, as pages cached before #13 post it), `week` and `replace` (an entry id: change that entry's outfit, below); 302 to the week (the outfit list's htmx dropdown and its 204 went with R5); malformed input, an unknown occasion included, is a 400); `POST /calendar/:id/delete` (`navigateTo` the week to htmx, else 303) and `/worn` (the pill fragment to htmx, else 303), whose body is optional and only picks the redirect: the week, or `returnTo` through `safeReturnTo` (Today's "Wore it" and its undo come back to `/`). Entries are the signed-in user's own: 404 for no such entry and for someone else's.
- **Scheduling is idempotent**: `UNIQUE (owner_id, day, outfit_id)`, and the one writer, `insertEntry` (`src/web/calendar/queries.ts`, taking a `Queryable`), inserts with `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE ... WHERE`, which changes one thing: **a person's write (`planned_by` `user`, the default) that meets the week planner's `auto` entry of that outfit on that day takes it over** (`planned_by` becomes `user`, #77; the occasion and the rest stay, the answer is still `already-scheduled`, with `adopted`, told apart from an insert by `xmax = 0` in `RETURNING`), and the planner's own `auto` write never downgrades a person's entry: POST /calendar through `scheduleOutfit` (ownership check first), the outfit form inside its save transaction, the gallery's pick (and through it "Plan my week", `planned_by` 'auto', see Weekly auto-plan), the seed. The unique index leads with `(owner_id, day)`, so it is also the index of the week query and of the owner foreign key.
- **Occasions** (#13, plan section 8): `outfit_calendar.occasion` (text, default and check constraint from `src/wardrobe/occasions.ts`; `drizzle/0013_outfit_calendar_occasion.sql` made every existing entry all day) is the part of the day an entry is for: `all-day`, `workout`, `work`, `daytime`, `evening`, `night-out`, in that display order (all day frames the day, the rest by when their windows start). `OCCASION_HINTS` gives each a weather window (hours in `APP_TIMEZONE`, e.g. work 8 to 18, evening 18 to 23) and a formality range on the garment scale (work 3 to 4, workout 1): the one place #14's weather and #9's generator read them from. **Several outfits a day are separate entries; the same outfit is on a day once**, whatever the occasion (the unique key did not change): planning it again for another occasion inserts nothing and keeps the first occasion, so an edit form re-saved with the picker at its default never moves an evening entry to all day. There is no "change an entry's occasion" write yet: delete the entry and plan it again. Labels are the catalog's `occasion.<value>` (`occasionLabel`, `src/web/calendar/labels.ts`, with the day and month names).
- **Changing an entry's outfit** (#69): `replaceEntryOutfit` (`replace.ts`), the one replace write, reached from POST /calendar's `replace` (the plan page's saved outfits), the gallery's pick with `replace` (Today's Change, the plan page's ideas) and `schedule_outfit`'s `replaceEntryId`. One transaction under `lockOwner`: the entry (the owner's, on that day and, when given, that occasion; else 404) locked, then the outfit (a saved one, `ownsOutfit`, or an idea through `pickIdea` without a plan), then `setEntryOutfit` (the entry keeps its id, day and occasion). The rules: **a worn entry is refused** (409 saying to add another outfit: it is the record of that day and its wears are that outfit's; Change is offered on unworn entries only); **its selfie is kept as a look on its day** (`detachEntrySelfie`: the photo shows the old outfit, the #19 rule for a deleted outfit); **the entry becomes the person's** (`planned_by` 'user'); **an outfit "Plan my week" created for it** goes once nothing holds it (`removeUnheldOutfits`, Undo's rule: no entry and no trip, `outfitIsHeld`), only while the entry was still `auto`; **the new outfit already on that day** is refused (409, naming its occasion: the unique key). Idempotent: a double tap's second request waits on the owner lock and finds the entry holding the outfit (`unchanged`; the pick's redirect says "Already saved"). Logs (context `Web`): `Calendar entry <id> (<day> <occasion>) changed by user <id>: outfit <a> -> [new ]outfit <b>[, selfie <id> kept as a look][, planner's outfit <a> removed]`, `... already holds outfit <id> ...; nothing changed`, `... not changed for user <id>: <reason>`. `test/integration/replace-entry.spec.ts`.
- **The page**: a day stacks its entries in occasion order (`buildCalendarView` sorts; `findEntries` reads by day and id) through **`OccasionRow`** (`occasion-row.tsx`: the label, Change (the plan page with `&replace=`, unworn entries only, #69), ×, an `OutfitCollage` thumb with the name linking to Styling, the selfie, the worn pill), the one way the week draws an entry. The page is R6's agenda (`calendar-page.tsx`): the Week/Month/Trips tabs, weather, "Plan my week", ‹ week ›, a strip of the week's days (a dot where something is planned) linking down to each day's `<section data-day>` (what the specs split days on, `test/integration/calendar-page.ts`): its weather chip, its rows, **open slots** (`OpenSlotRow`: the week template's occasions it has no outfit for, from today on; computed at hour 0 so `/calendar` stays byte-stable all day), its looks, then "+ Plan" (empty) or "+ Another outfit" **only on a day without open slots** (they open the same sheet, one way in per day). Today (#15) draws its entries larger but writes through the same worn route. **The month** (`month-page.tsx`, `GET /calendar/month`): a Sunday-to-Saturday grid, each day its first entry's collage, "+N" more and a worn dot, linking to its week scrolled to the day (`dayUrl`); the cell's collage drops accessories and bags (`bodyOf`: too narrow for the side column).
- **The plan page** (`plan-page.tsx`): the occasion as chip links (the choice lives in the URL), "Choose from ideas" (the gallery's Ideas with the same `?for=`, #9), "Style a new outfit" (Styling with the same `?for=`, #42), and the saved outfits as buttons of one native `PostForm` to POST /calendar (`data-needs-network`); an outfit already on the day is disabled, saying for which occasion. **The "+ Plan" sheet** (`plan-sheet.tsx`, a `<dialog>` per day after the week's sections, static so `/calendar` stays byte-stable): occasion radios, then Choose from ideas / Pick a saved outfit (the Saved tab, `/outfits?for=`, R5) / Style a new outfit, one GET form whose buttons' `formaction` all carry `?for=day:D&occasion=O`; its openers name the radio to check in `data-plan` (an open slot its occasion, the day's button all day) and share one one-line inline handler (`OPEN_PLAN_SHEET`) under the existing CSP. With `&replace=` of an unworn entry it is "Change outfit" (#69): no occasion chips (the entry's), and ideas, Styling and saved outfits all carrying `replace`; for a worn entry it plans another and says why.
- **`OutfitDestination`** (`src/web/outfits/destination.ts`, pure, unit-tested) is the one parser of `?for=day:YYYY-MM-DD&occasion=O[&replace=E]` (`parseDestination`, lenient: anything malformed is `none`, an unknown occasion all day, a malformed `replace` dropped) and its writer (`destinationQuery`). `replace` (#69, a day's only) names the entry a choice takes the place of; the writes check it (`replaceEntryOutfit`); an outfit opened in Styling to edit refuses it (it changes in place). The plan page, Styling (#42), Ideas (#9) and Today (#15) read or write it. Trips (#10) are `for=trip:ID[:YYYY-MM-DD][&occasion=O]` (no occasion is not all day there: a trip outfit may have none); `destinationTarget` writes the `for` value alone for a write's hidden field; Styling saves for a trip through `pickForTrip`, as the gallery's pick does.
- **Worn** is `setEntryWorn` (`src/web/wears/queries.ts`): the entry's `worn_at` and its wear rows change together (Wears and washes). Days after today have no pill and are refused. **"Wearing this"** for a whole outfit off the calendar (Today's "Wear this", a trip's "Wearing this today") is `wearOutfitOn` (`queries.ts`): planned on the day through `insertEntry` and marked worn, one transaction, idempotent.
- **Tabs**: `CalendarTabs` (Week, Month, Trips), static like the Outfits tabs (`/calendar` is a stale-while-revalidate tab root).
- **Selfies** (#19): each row (`OccasionRow`) shows its entry's selfie, or on today and before the camera and the library to take one (which marks it worn); a day also shows the looks kept after their outfit was deleted (`DayLooks`, read by `detachedLooks`). Deleting an entry goes through `removeEntry` (`writes.ts`), which unlinks its selfie after commit. See Outfit selfies.

## Gotchas

- **The calendar's unit specs (`src/web/calendar/*.spec.ts`) run in America/New_York through their own project**, `unit-new-york` in `vitest.config.ts` (`env: { TZ }`, `pool: 'forks'`), to prove the date logic ignores the process's zone. Vitest assigns `env` to the worker's real `process.env` before importing the spec, and Node reloads the zone on that assignment in a child process; worker threads keep the parent's zone, so the project must stay on forks. Each spec's first test asserts the offset. Another spec that needs a zone gets its own project the same way; it stays under `npm test` if its name starts with `unit`.
