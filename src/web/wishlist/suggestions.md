# Muse's suggestions and option groups

Linked from `CLAUDE.md` in this directory (Wishlist). Phase 1 of epic #332 (#333); the design is `docs/plans/2026-10-05-muse-suggestions.md`, which replaces the plans feature's UI. PR A is the model and its migration; PR B the inbox, the decision screen and the suggestion's page (below, "The screens").

## The model

- **A suggestion is a wishlist garment with provenance**, not a new status: `garment.suggested_at` marks one (it is what "a suggestion" means; the token can be null), with `suggested_by_token_id` (the agent's personal access token, `ON DELETE SET NULL`), `suggestion_group_id`, `suggestion_note` (the agent's reasoning) and `suggestion_rank` (1 to `MAX_OPTIONS_PER_GROUP`, 5). The check `garment_suggestion_check` allows none of them without `suggested_at`. Every "owned" check already leaves the wishlist out, so a suggestion is out of every closet read with no change there.
- **An option group** (`option_group`) is one need: its name, budget, the agent's note and token, `status` (`open`, `resolved`, `dismissed`), `resolved_garment_id` (what settled it: a pick chosen or bought, or "a different one" the owner bought, linked only from here), `dismissed_reason`, the owner's note and `decided_at` (null exactly while open, by a check). It may hold no picks: a need the agent is still looking for. `plan_item_id` is the plan item `drizzle/0040` made it from, for the plan tools' shims until plans go (#337).
- **Dismissal, never deletion**: `garment.dismissed_at`, `dismissed_reason` (`DISMISS_REASONS`: the owner's six, `too_pricey`, `colour`, `style`, `already_have`, `fit_size`, `not_now`, and the app's `chose_another` and `returned`; null only for the plans review's "Not this one", whose free-text reason is the note) and `dismissed_note`; a group's own reason and note when the need is turned down. A set-aside suggestion stays on the wishlist: it is the agent's feedback ("never propose this again").
- **Never deleted, enforced at the one delete writer**: `deleteGarment` (`src/web/wardrobe/queries.ts`) refuses a suggestion still on the wishlist (`{ ok: false, reason: 'suggestion' }`); a bought pick is an ordinary owned garment, deleted like any (its group's undo copes with the nulled `resolved_garment_id`). `DELETE /wardrobe/:id` answers it 409 (`wishlist.SUGGESTION_NOT_DELETED`); the plans review sets one aside through `decide` instead (`Not this one`: no reason, its text the note; an unpicked option `chose_another`; a declined item's `not_now`, `releasedCandidates`' `why`); "Bought it"'s plan follow-up leaves the siblings its purchase already set aside. Only the owner's account deletion removes one (the foreign key's cascade).
- **Provenance is kept once bought.** That is why "provenance only on a wishlist garment" cannot be a check constraint: it is the writer's rule (`markSuggestion`, below).

## One writer each

- **`decide(db, ownerId, decision)`** (`decisions.ts`) is every decision, its group the one the decision names (`dismiss-group`, `undo-group`, and `bought` for a different one carry `groupId`) or its garment's: This one (`choose`), Not for me on a pick (`dismiss-pick`) or a need (`dismiss-group`), their Undo (`undo-pick`, `undo-group`), a purchase settling the group (`bought`), and `returned` (which also archives the garment through `setGarmentStatus`). It holds the owner lock (so does `markSuggestion`, and no other writer touches these columns, so no row lock is taken), reads the group and its garments in one statement, asks the pure machine (`decideSuggestion`, `src/wardrobe/suggestions.ts`, whose header has the table of every decision) and writes the answer in one statement (data-modifying CTEs, through Drizzle's `getSQL()`). A refusal is `not-found` (another owner's, a 404) or `not-allowed` (a stale page or a double tap, a 409).
- **A choice and the siblings it sets aside share one instant**: the group's `decided_at` and their `dismissed_at` are both the transaction's `now()`, which is how Undo restores exactly those siblings and not ones the owner set aside before. Not for me on the chosen pick restores them too (the need is open again, its options with it); a group whose resolving garment was deleted since (the foreign key nulled it) can still be undone.
- **A purchase is never undone**; `returned` is its undo (the garment archived, dismissed `returned`, its group open again). A pick is restored only into an open group, so no resolved group shows open picks.
- **"Bought it"** (`buyCandidate`, `src/web/plans/purchase.ts`) calls `decide(bought)` in its own transaction: a bought suggestion resolves its group and sets the other open picks aside, the chosen one included; a pick bought after it was set aside (another was chosen) is cleared of its dismissal, the clean answer; a pick bought once another purchase settled the need (a different one, a sibling bought) is recorded the same way, but the first purchase stays the need's `resolved_garment_id`, never overwritten. `bought` settles a group only with a garment of the owner's in the closet (else `not-found` or `not-allowed`). A garment that is no suggestion finds no group and writes nothing.
- **`markSuggestion`** (`decisions.ts`) is provenance's writer, under the owner lock: only the owner's wishlist garment, only once, only into their own **open** group holding fewer than `MAX_OPTIONS_PER_GROUP` open options (`full` when only the room refused it; a refusal writes nothing). The agent's tools call it from #337; the garment form never writes these columns and a clone never copies them (`insertGarment` takes form fields).

## What reads what

- **Closet reads keep `inCloset`**: no suggestion, in any state, is in the grid, Styling, capsules, laundry, tagging, Ideas, Today, insights or MCP's closet tools, and none can be worn, washed, lent or put in an outfit.
- **Lists of things to buy read `wanted`** (`src/web/wardrobe/status.ts`: on the wishlist and not set aside): the Wishlist tab, `list_wishlist`, plans' candidate readers (the shopping list, Today's next purchase, covers, Styling's To-buy row; and a plan look's pieces, `candidateIdsOf` and `lookSlotsSql`, so a look holding one set aside shows it missing). A garment's own page, "Bought it" and goes-with keep `onWishlist`. The plans' candidate cap counts `wanted` picks too, so one set aside (its link kept) frees its slot.

## The screens (PR B; the doc's section 4 D, C, F)

Code: `inbox.ts` (the reads), `inbox-page.tsx` (D), `group-page.tsx` (C), `suggestion-section.tsx` (F's part), `suggestion-parts.tsx` (the forms and lines they share), `inbox-routes.tsx` (every route below). A user-facing "need" is an `option_group`.

- **The inbox, `GET /wardrobe/wishlist`** (`PageMain wide`; one markup, `CANDIDATE_GRID` and `PAIR_GRID`): Ready to buy (needs resolved by a pick still on the wishlist; Bought it the primary), Muse's picks (open needs with a wanted option: a card each, its options' photos with price and "Unlocks N" (said once in the facts, "Unlocks 50+ outfits each", when every option unlocks the same: `sharedUnlocks`; C does the same), the card one stretched link to C, a thumb `?option=` centring it; most unlocks first, then Muse's rank; `GROUPS_PAGE_SIZE` 10, then the Ideas sentinel `GET /wardrobe/wishlist/more?page=`, which counts every open pick's unlocks again to sort before it slices its page: within the budget below, the price of a sort no single page can know), Your wishlist (wishlist garments outside any need: own items, and a lone suggestion badged "From Muse" until phase 3's singles), "Muse is still looking for" (open needs with no wanted option, each with a one-tap `not_now`), and "N set aside" (`<details>`, shown under the empty state too: needs set aside, and picks set aside of open needs or none, each with Undo; the siblings a choice set aside come back with its Undo, on C). Each need and garment is in exactly one section (`sectionsOf`).
- **Reads**: the inbox and C are 2 statements (the session, one `selectScalars`: the needs with their picks and resolver as JSON, own items, the switcher, brand notes, and the owner's "Unlocks N" inputs, `goesWithManyInputsSql`: every wanted pick of an open need locked against **one** closet read). Unlocks is `outfitCount` per pick (`unlocksOf`), C's `bestOutfits` per option (count and best 3, without goesWith's per-layer searches). Measured (2026-10-05, demo closet of 80): about 3 ms a pick, p95 57 ms at production's 16 options, 362 ms at the worst case 24 needs × 5; no memo. Each page logs its search time at debug. F stays 3: `suggestionContextSql` (provenance, its need with its picks) joins `garmentContext`'s statement when `suggested_at` is set.
- **New from Muse** (doc section 9: never in the bare page, which may be a cached copy): the owner's inbox carries an empty `#muse-new` that posts `POST /wardrobe/wishlist/seen` on load (`hx-swap="none"`); the answer is that slot out of band, naming the open needs with a wanted pick suggested after `user.suggestions_seen_at` (every one when null), and moves it to the newest of them, one statement (`markSuggestionsSeen`). A POST never passes through the worker's cache. `muse-inbox.spec.ts` holds the bare page byte for byte the same before and after a seen POST that moves the marker.
- **Decisions**: `POST /wardrobe/:id/choose`, `/dismiss` (`reason`: one of `OWNER_DISMISS_REASONS`, else 400; `note`, trimmed), `/undo`; `POST /wardrobe/wishlist/needs/:id/dismiss`, `/undo` (a need set aside, or a choice). Each is one `decide`, the owner's alone (`authorizeWardrobe` `own`: a grantee with `?ownerId=` a 403, without it the id is not in their wardrobe, decide's 404; a stale page 409), a `PostForm` with `needsNetwork`, answering 303 to its `returnTo` (`safeReturnTo`) with `?decided=` for the toast (`decisionToastOf`, URL state). "Not for me" opens in place: a note textarea, then a submit button per reason. Returned it is the garment page's ⋯ (a bought suggestion), an `hx-post` like Archive answering `navigateTo`.
- **C, `GET /wardrobe/wishlist/needs/:id`**: the options in a `SnapStrip` of size `options` (85% on a phone; side-by-side columns from `lg`, see Snap strip), each card: the photo (the shared viewer, set `need-options`), price against the budget (`budgetFit`), Unlocks N, the brand note, Muse's note clamped, **This one**, then View product, Details (F) and Not for me, and its best 3 outfits (`OutfitCollage` `cell`, the pick marked to buy). Chosen: the pick alone, Bought it, Bought a different one and Undo choice. Set aside: Undo the primary. Foot: Bought a different one and Not this need right now. A grantee reads it with no forms and no unlocks.
- **F** (`garment-page.tsx`, `SuggestionSection`): "From Muse", the need (a link to C), price against its budget, the note, the owner's decisions by state (open: This one; chosen: Bought it; set aside: Undo) and the need's other open options; Bought a different one is the need's, so on C only. The owner's `PrimaryActions` drops its Bought it on a suggestion (one primary); ⋯ hides Remove from wishlist on one still wished for (Not for me is the way) and adds Returned it once bought. Back goes to C for a grouped pick.
- **Bought a different one**: `GET /wardrobe/new?forNeed=<id>` is the closet form prefilled from the need's best pick (`destinationValues`, as a replacement's), with a banner; the post carries `forNeed` and `decide(bought, groupId)` runs in the garment's own transaction (`postedDestination`'s `withGarment`). Only a need still to buy for (open, or chosen and the pick unbought: the machine's `stillToBuy`, checked by `findNeedToBuyFor` before anything is stored, a 404, and by `decide` again under the owner lock, a 409, so two at once settle it once and the other's garment rolls back). "Bought it" on a suggestion shows no plans' follow-ups.
- **Plans unlinked** (owner decision, 2026-10-05): no Plans tab, no ⋯ Plans or Shopping list, no candidacy links on wishlist cards. Today's card is Muse's needs to decide (`needsToDecideSql`) leading here; the next-purchase card links the product's page. The plans routes and MCP tools work until #337.
- **Offline**: the warm list holds the inbox, its needs' C pages (open and chosen) and the wanted wishlist garments' pages and thumbs, within `WARM_WISHLIST_CAP` (60, added to the page and image caps); a set-aside one is only kept. **Export**: `suggestion_group_id` is exported, the JSON gains `optionGroups` (`EXPORT_GROUP_COLUMNS`: not the owner, token or plan item), the CSV an `option_group` column with the need's name.

## The migration (`drizzle/0040_muse-suggestions.sql`)

Copies from the plan tables and never changes them, so the plans pages and tools keep working until #337. **Only plans the agent drafted** (`drafted_by_token_id` set; owner decision, 2026-10-05): an owner's own plan, the demo persona's included, is left alone and its candidates stay plain wishlist items. Each drafted plan's item becomes a group (its name, else its type or category; budget, note, owner's note; the drafted plan's token); a declined item is a dismissed need (`not_now`), every other review state open (matching targets, quantity, priority and proposed vs accepted are retired by the design). Each candidate's garment becomes its group's suggestion (the link's note, rank and time); a garment linked to several items belongs to the lowest plan id's (a duplicated plan copies its links; the original is older), then the lowest item id's; an item every candidate of which belongs to another plan's item is that item's copy and is left out, while two items of one plan sharing a garment both stay (the later without it). A group with a candidate already bought is resolved by it, its other open picks set aside at the same instant. A rejection (its garment deleted at the time) comes back as a photo-less dismissed suggestion, its free-text reason as the note and no reason of the fixed set, and its URL dropped if it is not http(s) (the garment's check would refuse it; a cosmetic field never fails a boot). It aborts the boot only on a candidate of another owner: no app path makes one, so it is corruption to surface, not skip.

Production on 2026-10-05: the owner's draft plan 14 (24 proposed items: 10 with no candidate, 4 with 1, 8 with 2, 2 with 3; 26 candidates, every link without a rank or note; no rejections) becomes 24 open groups, 10 of them still looking; the demo persona's hand-made plan 13 (19 accepted items, 2 candidates) is untouched. A drafted plan's item with no candidates of its own in a duplicated draft still becomes its own group (production has none). Its 8 looks are phase 2's. **Rollback**: the change is additive, so the previous image boots on it (drizzle applies nothing it does not know); only migrated rejections would show on its wishlist, removed by deleting the garments with `suggested_at` and `dismissed_at` set and no photo (an owner-approved write, in a transaction).
