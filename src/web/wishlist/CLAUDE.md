# Wishlist

`src/wardrobe/status.ts`, `src/web/wardrobe/status.ts`, `src/web/wishlist/` (#18, slice 18a; plan section 11). A wishlist item is a garment with `status = 'wishlist'`: same table, form, photo, link import and sharing as any garment, and out of every closet read.

- **The status is a state machine.** `garment.status` (`wishlist`, `closet`, `archived`; a check constraint) replaced `archived boolean` in `drizzle/0014_garment_status.sql` (true to archived, false to closet; `garment-status-migration.spec.ts`). Transitions are events, each with one edge: `buy` wishlist to closet, `archive` closet to archived, `restore` archived to closet (`garmentStatusTransition`, pure, `status.spec.ts` covers all nine pairs). Refused on purpose: wishlist to archived (dropping a wishlist item is a delete: it has no history to keep), anything to wishlist (wanting another is a new item: clone or "Find a replacement"), and self-moves. A garment is inserted with an entry status (`insertGarment`'s `EntryStatus`: closet or wishlist; a clone of a wishlist item lands on the requester's wishlist, anything else in their closet, `statusOfClone`) and changed afterwards **only by `setGarmentStatus`** (row locked, the machine asked, a purchase's day and price written with it): archive, restore, "Bought it" (`buyGarment`, which archives the replaced garment in the same transaction only when asked) and the seed.
- **`inCloset` is the one predicate of every closet read** (`src/web/wardrobe/status.ts`, with `onWishlist`, `ownedGarment` = closet or archived, and `inScope` for `GridFilters.scope`): the grid (`ownedGarment` with "Show archived"), its filter choices, the builder's cycles, capsules' counts, strips and pages, tagging, laundry and the wash counts, `availableGarment`/`isAvailable`, the MCP tools' closet reads, insights (#17). Writes that may only name owned garments filter by `ownedGarment`: outfit slots (`insertSlots`), capsule membership (`changeMembership`), `markWashed`, `setAway`; `setWoreToday` answers `'wishlist'`; the wear routes answer a wishlist item 409. The outfit generator (#9) reads `availableGarment`, which includes it; "Goes with my closet" (#18b) reads `closetGarments` (`inCloset`) and locks the wishlist item beside it (see below). `wishlist.spec.ts` proves an item is absent from each read.
- **Replacing**: `garment.replaces_garment_id` (nullable, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, not itself by a check), kept after "Bought it" as the record of what the purchase replaced. The same-owner rule is the writers': `replacementOf` (`src/web/wardrobe/queries.ts`) stores the id through a subquery in the insert or update itself, only when it names a garment of the same owner that is owned now or once, else null. It is read from the form only beside its marker `wishlist=1` (Gotchas). A garment marked `replace_soon` shows "Find a replacement" (owner and MANAGE) to `/wardrobe/new?to=wishlist&replaces=<id>`, prefilled from it (`destinationValues`: category, type, properties, brand, colours, size; not its name, notes, dates, price or care), and lists the wishlist items that would replace it.
- **Routes**: `GET /wardrobe/wishlist` (the Wardrobe's Wishlist tab in `wardrobe-header.tsx`; under `/wardrobe`, so `sections.ts` already marks it; unpaged, a wishlist is a handful), `GET|POST /wardrobe/:id/bought` (the day, today in `APP_TIMEZONE`, and the price paid, the listed one, prefilled; a checkbox to archive the replaced garment when it is still in the closet and the requester is its owner, unchecked; a native post re-rendered 400 on a bad date or price; 303 to the garment with `?bought=1`; an item no longer on the wishlist is a 409, its form a 302 to the garment). New items come from the garment form (`GET /wardrobe/new?to=wishlist`, posting `to=wishlist`; no care fields or acquired date on a wishlist form) and from the link import, which carries `?to=wishlist&replaces=` from the link page to the form (`resolveDestination`, `src/web/wardrobe/destination.ts`; the share target stays the closet).
- **Shared like the rest of the wardrobe** (owner decision on #18): a VIEW grantee reads the owner's wishlist (gifting; the owner reading a sibling's), a MANAGE grantee adds, edits and buys, only the owner archives what a purchase replaces (a grantee asking is a 403) and deletes an item. Matrix rows in `authorization-wardrobe.spec.ts`.
- **"Goes with my closet" (#18b; `src/wardrobe/goes-with.ts` pure, `goesWithCloset` in `src/web/gallery/ideas.ts`, `gallery/goes-with.tsx`)**: a wishlist item's page judges it against the closet, **the owner's alone** (it reads their closet and clashes, like ideas; a grantee who reads the item never gets it, and `goes_with_closet` refuses another's item: matrix rows in `authorization-wardrobe.spec.ts`, `mcp-seed.spec.ts`). The generator with the item `locked` (never in a pool, so ideas, Today and picks cannot draw it: `wishlistGarments` is the only read that hands one to the generator) over `closetGarments`: **the whole closet**, dirty and away included (a purchase is judged against what is owned, not today's laundry), **no weather** (worn across seasons; so no layer is drawn and each closet layer is checked on its own, locked with the item), **the item's own formality as the range** (the best few are those dressed at its level; the count still takes every outfit the hard rules allow), **no rotation** (idle days nulled: uniform draws). One search asks for a page as large as `MAX_DRAWS`, so it returns every distinct outfit it met: the count is exact below `OUTFIT_COUNT_CAP` (50) and "50+" past it; the best 3 as `OutfitCollage` cards (display only: no form, `pickIdea` takes closet garments; "Bought it" changes that); per role it is worn with, "k of n" and the best 3 partners (at its formality, then in the most outfits), layers always asked ("No layer goes with it"); **near-duplicates** (`nearDuplicates`: same category, same type, same colour set, not empty; brands ignored, where #20's "add a copy" asks it with `sameBrand`) warn "do I need this?", except the garment it replaces, shown like for like. Seeded by the item's id. The shopping list's candidate cards show the count as a chip loaded when it scrolls into view (`OutfitCountSlot`, `hx-trigger="revealed"`, from `GET /wardrobe/:id/outfit-count`: `goesWithCount`, the owner's own wishlist item, else a 404; always a fragment): three statements and one search, 4 to 8 ms warm and about 20 ms cold over Theo's closet. Not inline, on purpose: the list's worst case would be its items (a plan's are unbounded) × `MAX_CANDIDATES_PER_ITEM` (5) × up to ~20 ms, already about 2 s at 20 items, past the ~500 ms a page may take; lazy, the page costs what the list costs and each chip pays for itself once seen. Theo's merino (replaces T21), padded jacket and Allbirds (twin of the Vejas) are the demo (`34-demo-goes-with`). ~15-35 ms over his 80 garments, so it renders inline; the page logs one debug line.
- **Plans' part (#34b)**: on the owner's own wishlist each card names the plan items it is a candidate for, with "For plan item…", and the header links the shopping list; the owner's "Bought it" shows what the purchase does to those items and takes their follow-ups (`adjustItems`, `removeCandidates`; `buyCandidate`, see Wardrobe plans). A grantee sees none of it and asking for a follow-up is a 403, like the archive.

## Gotchas

- **A wishlist form's `replaces` has its own marker, `wishlist=1`.** Closet forms and forms cached before #18 post neither and must leave the stored replacement alone (the product, care and props markers' rule). The replacement is stored only through `replacementOf`, which is where the same-owner rule lives: a second writer of `replaces_garment_id` must go through it.
