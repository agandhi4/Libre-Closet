# Sizes

`src/wardrobe/measurements.ts`, `src/wardrobe/brands.ts` (pure), `src/web/sizes/`, `src/web/mcp/tools/sizes.ts` (#24; plan section 16). **The signed-in user's own, like the style profile**: no route takes `?ownerId=`, another user's brand row is a 404 (matrix rows in `authorization-account.spec.ts`).

- **Tables** (`drizzle/0026_sizes.sql`): `body_measurements` (one row per user: eight optional lengths **in cm**, numeric(5, 2), 1 to 300 by a check, and `unit`, `in` by default, how they are read and typed: the weather's temperature pattern, its own preference) and `brand_size` (`brand`, the spelling shown, and `brand_key`, its `brandKey`, computed in JS by the writers; one row per brand however it is typed, `brand_size_user_id_brand_key_unique`; a size normalized like a garment's and a note, at least one by a check). One writer each (`queries.ts`): `saveMeasurements`, `setLengthUnit`, `addBrandSize`/`updateBrandSize` (a violation of the index is `'brand-taken'`), `deleteBrandSize`.
- **Brands are compared through `brandKey`, the one normaliser** (trimmed, spaces collapsed, NFC, case folded so "ß"/"ẞ"/"SS" and a decomposed "é"/"İ" agree): the notes' index and lookups (`brandSizeFor` by `brand_key = brandKey(input)`, `brandSizeLookup` for a list) and insights' brand breakdown. A new brand comparison uses it; never SQL's `lower()`, whose collation disagrees with it on non-ASCII.
- **The unit never moves a length**: the measurements form posts the unit its numbers were shown in (hidden `unit`), and a value posted as shown keeps the stored cm (`readMeasurementsForm`), so switching units and saving drifts nothing. Inches show two decimals (quarter inches round-trip), cm one.
- **Routes**: `GET /auth/profile/sizes` (the editor; `?saved=` a toast), `POST /auth/profile/sizes/unit`, `/measurements`, `/brands`, `/brands/:id`, `/brands/:id/delete` (native posts, 303 back or the editor 400 with the field's message), `GET /auth/profile/sizes/hint?brand=` (always a fragment: the requester's own note).
- **Where a note shows, only on the requester's own wardrobe**: Profile › Sizes (read-only, "Edit sizes"), under Size on the garment form (every mode, the link import's prefilled form included; the brand field refreshes `#brand-size-hint` as it is typed, `BRAND_SIZE_HINT_TRIGGER`), the Wishlist tab's cards and a wishlist item's page. On a shared wardrobe there is no hint at all, not even the grantee's own (it describes the wrong body): a new page that shows a note checks `viewOwner === undefined` / `access.isOwner`. MCP: `get_sizes` (read only, the token's user).
- **Seed**: Theo's His sizes tables (Measurements, Brand sizes) through the editor's readers; Dana and Riley have none.
