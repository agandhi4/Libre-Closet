# Wardrobe

`src/web/wardrobe/` (on Drizzle since 2026-09-26): `routes.tsx`, `render-form.tsx` (the garment form page, shared with the link import), `queries.ts` (plain rows), `writes.ts` (the photo-aware writes), `validation.ts`, `garment.ts` (the category and size rules the outfit builder shares), `urls.ts`, `link-import/` (see Link import), and the pages (`wardrobe-page.tsx`, `garment-page.tsx`, `garment-form.tsx`, `wardrobe-header.tsx`: every Wardrobe tab's app bar, the switcher as its title, the ⋯ menu (`WardrobeMenu`: Select and Tag garments for an editor, Plans, Shopping list, Insights, and on one's own wardrobe the year in review) and the ＋ add sheet (to the closet or the wishlist, from a link or by hand, and New capsule on the owner's Capsules tab; to the closet also Take a photo (`capture=environment`) and Choose from library, #97: each a label over a file input joined through `form=` to its own native multipart `PostForm`, prepared on the phone by `PREPARE_AND_SUBMIT_PHOTO`, "Uploading…" while one submits; see Routes), and the Closet/Capsules/Laundry/Wishlist tabs, Laundry left out of a shared wardrobe), `status.ts` (`inCloset` and the status writer; see Wishlist), `destination.ts` (a new garment's closet or wishlist destination). Every route takes `?ownerId=` for a shared wardrobe and resolves it once through `authorizeWardrobe` (`src/web/sharing/access.ts`, over `resolveWardrobeAccess`; the capsule routes use it too): reads need a view, writes a MANAGE share or ownership, archive and delete ownership, a clone only a view (it lands in the requester's own wardrobe). A garment outside the addressed wardrobe is a 404 like a missing id.

- **Routes**: `GET /wardrobe` (the page, or `#wardrobe-main` alone to an htmx fragment request, with `Vary`), `GET /wardrobe/tiles` (the next grid page; always a fragment), `GET /wardrobe/new`, `POST /wardrobe/properties-fragment` (the form's properties after a category, type or weight change; see Properties), `POST /wardrobe/bulk` (select mode's "Set…"; see Properties), `GET /wardrobe/tag` and `POST /wardrobe/:id/tag` (tagging mode; see Tagging mode), `GET /wardrobe/:id`, `GET /wardrobe/:id/edit`, `GET|POST /wardrobe/:id/clone`, `POST /wardrobe`, `POST /wardrobe/:id` (every field; a cleared one is NULL), `POST /wardrobe/:id/photo` (the photo sheet's native multipart `photo`, queued for its cutout; a `nobgPhoto` part from an old cached page is ignored; 303 to `?photoSaved=1`, `HX-Redirect` to a page cached while it was an hx-post; a refusal is the error page), `POST /wardrobe/:id/nobg` (the mask editor's fetch, answers `{ version }`; the `edit` event), `GET /wardrobe/:id/cutout` (the photo fragment the pending page polls) and `POST /wardrobe/:id/cutout/retry` (native post, 303 back), `POST /wardrobe/:id/archive` and `/restore` (each names its move: a status that does not take it is a 409, never the opposite move the old toggle made) and `DELETE /wardrobe/:id` (`navigateTo` the grid, or the wishlist for a wishlist item; owner only); `GET /wardrobe/new?to=wishlist[&replaces=<id>]` (the wishlist's form; see Wishlist), `POST /wardrobe/:id/condition` (the garment page's condition control, an `AutosaveForm`, owner and MANAGE; answers its status line, a plain post 303). The wear, wash and away posts are the wears plugin's (Wears and washes). Adding from a link (`link-import/routes.tsx`, its own plugin; owner or MANAGE): `GET /wardrobe/new/from-link` (the link page; also the manifest's `share_target`, which sends `title`, `text`, `url`), `POST /wardrobe/new/from-link` (fetch, then the garment form prefilled, 200), `POST /wardrobe/new/from-link/photo` (another of the page's photos, or none; answers the photo slot). Adding from a photo (#97, owner or MANAGE): `POST /wardrobe/new/photo` (the add sheet's multipart `photo`, access checked before the body is read, so VIEW's 403 and a stranger's 404 store nothing; `stagePhotoUpload` stores it through `storeUploadParts`, as every garment photo, and keeps it as the requester's pending photo; 303 to `GET /wardrobe/new?photo=<name>`, which shows it only while it is still the requester's own pending photo, else "no longer available", also where a back navigation after saving lands). `POST /wardrobe` takes the form's `linkPhoto`, the pending photo whichever way it came.
- **The garment page** (#84, `garment-page.tsx`): the hero (cutout, else original, contained on the plinth at 4:5, with the mask editor's pencil), the facts line, then the owner's wear line (`wear-section.tsx`: cost per wear from `perWearCost`, `src/wardrobe/insights.ts`, the one rule insights uses too, so the two never disagree; Style this, Wore today, Washed), the property chips, the capsules row, "In N outfits" (`garment-outfits.tsx`: the count and the newest `GARMENT_OUTFITS_SHOWN` (8) as thumb collages), then details and care (the care label, condition, and the owner's "Where it is", `#garment-away`, and repair log; `care-and-repairs.md`). **Owner-only data (wears, washes, outfits, cost, avoided pairs, the repair log) is read only by `ownerRecords` (`routes.tsx`)**, never for a grantee or a wishlist item; the page renders what the model holds, so a new owner-only read belongs there, not in a view. The garment form's owner-only field (Wash after) has its one rule too: `formAudience` / `FormAudience` (see Wears and washes, Garment form). Everything else is the app bar's ⋯ menu (`GarmentMenu`, buttons and links only: a form in a daisyUI menu item loses its styling): Edit, Photo and Share for owner and MANAGE, Clone for anyone who sees it, archive/restore and delete for the owner. Photo opens the photo sheet (`#garment-photo-sheet`, also the empty hero's button), whose inputs keep the ids `#photoInput` and `#photoCaptureInput` (the e2e and integration specs select them).
- **The grid is keyset-paged**: `GRID_PAGE_SIZE` (48) tiles newest first, `id < before` for the next page, one statement per page that reads only what a tile shows (id, name, category, status, photo name and version). The last tile is followed by a sentinel (`hx-trigger="revealed"`, `hx-swap="outerHTML"`) that fetches `/wardrobe/tiles` with the same filters and replaces itself with the next page and its own sentinel. Filtering and searching swap `#wardrobe-main` and so start at page one; the result count is a separate `count(*)` over the same filters. Indexes: `(owner_id, status, id desc)` and `(owner_id, category, id desc)`, also the owner foreign key's; at household size the planner often prefers a backward primary-key scan with a filter, which is as fast (under 0.3 ms at 1,500). A new sort order needs its own cursor and index.
- **Care filters and badges** (#7): `?needsWash=true` (a copy needs a wash; the owner's own wardrobe only, `gridSearch` drops it on a share, and the modal does not offer it there) and `?attention=true` (condition not good), checkboxes in the modal's Care group. Tiles carry `quantity` ("×3") and `condition` for everyone, and `care` (dirty copies, away) only when `gridPage(..., { ownerView })` is the owner's: the correlated wear count is not even computed for a share. The owner's grid shows a slim "N garments need a wash · Laundry" banner (`countNeedingWash`); Laundry is also a Wardrobe tab, not on a shared wardrobe.
- **Capsule filter** (`?capsule=<id>`, #8): members only (`inCapsule`, `src/web/capsules/queries.ts`); chosen from the scope row's capsule menu (`CapsuleScope`, which also links the Capsules tab), carried by every link through `GridSearch`; a `?capsule=` URL is honoured wherever it comes from. Not an id is a 400, not one of the addressed wardrobe's capsules a 404.
- **Property filters**: `type` (only with its category: `gridSearch` drops a type the chosen category lacks, so dropping the category pill drops it too), `warmth`, `formality` and `material` (`materials @> array[...]`, as the colour's `colors @> array[...]`); the scales and the material are 400 outside their sets, like a colour. No GIN index on either array: the owner's grid index narrows to one wardrobe (hundreds of rows) and the containment is a filter over those, as the other property filters are. The filter modal offers only values the wardrobe holds (`filterOptions`, one statement; materials through an `unnest` subquery). `GridSearch`'s keys are the query parameters' names, so `searchParams(search)` is the whole list: links, the sentinel, the search form's hidden inputs and the bulk form's action all spread it, and a new filter needs no second list.
- **Select mode, bulk edit and tagging mode**: `select-and-tag.md` in this directory; read it before touching `?select=1`, `/wardrobe/bulk`, `/wardrobe/tag` or `tag-page.tsx`.
- **The care label and the repair log** (#23): `care-and-repairs.md` in this directory; read it before touching the `care*` properties, `?wash=`, `repairs.ts`, `repair-log.tsx` or `/wardrobe/:id/repairs`.
- **The duplicate check, "add a copy"** (#20): `lookalikes.md` in this directory; read it before touching `lookalikes.ts`, `lookalike-region.tsx`, `/wardrobe/lookalikes` or `/wardrobe/:id/copies`.
- **Search** is `ILIKE` on name, notes and brand with the keyword's `%`, `_` and `\` escaped (`containsPattern`). The colour filter (`colors @> array[<colour>]`) must be a built-in colour (else 400).
- **The form is validated in two layers** (`validation.ts`): TypeBox caps shape and length (a 400 error page; the inputs carry the same `maxlength`), then `readGarmentForm` re-renders the form with a 400 and messages for a blank category, a colour outside `GARMENT_COLORS` or a date that is not one. Categories are stored trimmed and lower case (the filter value and the builder's key), one-line fields trimmed, blank fields NULL, sizes normalized (`XL` is `X-Large`). The date field keeps its historical name `dateAquired` (cached pages post it); the column is `acquired_on date`.
- **Colours are a fixed set** (`GARMENT_COLORS`, `src/wardrobe/properties.ts` since #34, so the schema's plan and style checks can list them; each has a `.ms-swatch--<name>` class). **`garment.colors` is a `text[]` set** (#28), one model with `materials` and `plan_item.colors`: in `GARMENT_COLORS` order, each once, null for none (never an empty array), checked by `garment_colors_check`; every writer of a set column normalises through `storedSet` (`properties.ts`: the list's order, each once, null for none; `readColors` and `readProperties` for the garment form, bulk edit's material, the plan and style forms, "Bought it"'s fitted item), and readers take `colors ?? []`, never a string to split. `drizzle/0023_garment_colors.sql` converted the comma-joined `color` column (trimmed, lower case, blanks dropped, reordered, duplicates dropped) after aborting the boot, naming the rows, on any item outside the set. The form's field is still `color` (one per checked box), so forms the installed app cached post what they always did and need no marker; the MCP tools' output was already `colors: string[]`. `public/js/color-multiselect.js` only mirrors the server-rendered checkboxes as pills, built with `createElement`/`textContent`: a stored colour rendered through `innerHTML` was a stored XSS (audit2-correctness H5), and free-text colours are gone with it.
- **Properties** (#12; plan section 6): `type`, `warmth` (1-5), `formality` (1-4), `materials` (`text[]`, a set, null for none), `pattern`, `fit`, `sleeve`, `length`, `fabric_weight` (gsm, entered in oz or gsm; 6 oz is 203) and `water_resistant`, all optional, each with a check constraint listing `src/wardrobe/properties.ts`'s values. The form renders only what the category's role has (`propertyApplies`: no sleeve on shoes; a custom category gets the general ones and no type) and a save stores null for the rest, so recategorising drops what no longer applies. Type, warmth and weight sit beside the category; the rest in the collapsed "More details". Changing the category or anything in either block posts the form to `POST /wardrobe/properties-fragment` (reads and writes nothing; the triggers are `autosaveAttributes`, see Gotchas), which answers both blocks' contents (`#garment-props-main`'s in place, `#garment-props-more`'s out of band; never the blocks, which carry the triggers) with **presets filled only where the user has not chosen**: the form carries hidden `presetCategory`/`presetType`/`presetWeight` (where the shown presets came from), and `applyPresets` moves a value to the new preset only when it is unset or still equals the old one. A heavier fabric of the same type is warmer (`weightSteps`: a 6 oz tee is warmth 3). Labels are the catalog's `property.<name>.<value>` strings through `tKey` (`labels.ts`; `labels.spec.ts` proves every value has one).
- **Product link and price** (`source_url` text, `price` numeric(10,2); added for the seed and link import #6): optional fields in "More details", read by `readSourceUrl` (an absolute http(s) URL, stored as the parser writes it; anything else is a message, so a `javascript:` link never reaches the page) and `readPrice` (`$1,299` and `49.9` read as `1299.00` and `49.90`; never negative), both backed by check constraints. The garment page shows the price (`priceLabel`: US dollars, the household's one currency) and a "View product" link with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`. A clone keeps both.
- **Link import**: `link-import/CLAUDE.md`.
- **Photo writes** (`writes.ts`): the garment is looked up before a multipart body is read, so a refused upload stores nothing; `Photos.storeUploadParts` writes the bytes; `commitWithPhoto` inserts the `file` row with the garment write in one transaction and deletes the new bytes if it fails; replaced or deleted photos are unlinked after commit.

## Gotchas

- **A garment form without properties must not clear them.** An edit writes every field, and a form the installed app cached before properties existed posts none. Every form that renders them posts `props=1` (a hidden field in `PropertiesMain`); `readGarmentForm` reads and writes properties only with it (`garment-properties.spec.ts` saves an old-shaped form over a tagged garment). The product link and price have their own marker, `product=1` (a hidden field beside them in "More details"), because forms cached between the two post `props=1` without them (`garment-product.spec.ts`). The care fields (quantity, wash after, condition, #7) have `care=1`, the care label (#23) `careLabel=1`. A new garment field that old cached forms lack needs the same care: a marker of its own, posted beside the fields it guards.
- **A garment's status changes only through `setGarmentStatus`, and closet reads only through `inCloset`.** A new query that lists "the wardrobe" without the predicate shows wishlist items (things not owned) in the closet, the builder or laundry; one that writes `garment.status` directly skips the machine's refusals. `insertGarment` takes an entry status (closet or wishlist, never archived). The grid's `?archived=true` is the URL contract kept from before the status; internally it is `scope: 'owned'`.
