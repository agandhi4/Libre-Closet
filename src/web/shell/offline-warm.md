# Warming the wardrobe for offline reading (#286)

Linked from `CLAUDE.md` in this directory (PWA and the service worker). The design and its numbers: `docs/plans/2026-09-28-caching-and-offline.md`, sections 2 and 5. Read this before touching `WARM_PAGES`, `GET /offline/warm` or what the worker keeps for offline reading.

**What.** The installed app keeps the signed-in account's own wardrobe readable offline: the four tab roots, this week and the next (`/calendar?week=<next Sunday>`), every closet garment's page, every outfit's page, the closet grid's later pages (`/wardrobe/tiles?before=`, as htmx requests, so under their `|hx` key) and every thumb those pages show. Not warmed (owner decisions on #178): cutouts (offline, a cutout never viewed is drawn from its thumb: `cutoutStandIn` answers a failed `/file/nobg/<name>?…` with the cached `/file/thumb/<name>?…`, the same query), selfies, Today, shared wardrobes. Writes stay online only.

**The list.** `GET /offline/warm` (`routes.tsx`, `warm-list.ts`): session-gated, `no-store`, one statement (`selectScalars`: the closet and wishlist garments, and `savedOutfitsSql`). Always the session's own wardrobe, never `?ownerId=`, so warming never widens what a grantee's device holds. Caps: 300 garments, 80 outfits, newest first (`WARM_GARMENT_CAP`, `WARM_OUTFIT_CAP`). It answers `{ pages, fragments, images, keep }` (`WarmList`, `offline-warm.ts`, which the worker bundles; `parseWarmList` checks it at the worker's boundary) and says whose list it is in `X-Page-Account`. `keep` lists the garment and outfit pages that still exist but are not warmed (wishlist items, pieces past the caps): their visited copies stay. The grid pages are built by `tilesUrl` (`wardrobe-page.tsx`), the sentinel's own builder, so the cached copy is the one the offline scroll asks for (`offline-warm.spec.ts` in `test/integration/` compares them).

**The run** (`runWarm`, `src-sw.ts`):

1. `pwa.js` posts `WARM_PAGES` on a signed-in page after `load`, and again when the app comes back visible or online: never hidden, offline (the heartbeat's state) or with `saveData`. Asking is cheap: the worker decides.
2. The `warmed-at` record (`page-cache.invalid`, in `pages-v2`) gates it to once a day. A drop takes it with the cache, so the first page after a sign-in warms the new account.
3. The list is fetched (`X-Closet-Warm: 1`) and the session caches are claimed for its account (`claimPageCache`): another account's are dropped first. A list answered by the login page claims them for nobody, like a page landing there.
4. Cached `/wardrobe/:id` and `/outfits/:id` pages (`isWarmedDetailPage`: no query, so never a shared wardrobe's) on neither `pages` nor `keep` are deleted: deleted or archived since. Under the ownership lock, and only while the run's generation lasts.
5. Due: a page without a copy, or one older than 24 h, another account's, or stored before this worker activated; a thumb not cached. Pages and thumbs alternate, two at a time, each marked `X-Closet-Warm: 1` (the server's request log says `(warm)`), through the caches' own strategies: `pageWarmer` (`PageRefresh`: the network's copy or a failure, never NetworkFirst's cached fallback) over `pagePlugins`, and `images` (CacheFirst, `imageStore`). So every plugin applies: the 200 filter, `isRenderedPage`, the owner claim, the stamp, and the generation check that refuses an answer to a request started before a drop.
6. It stops at a drop (`the session changed`), at the first fetch that fails (`unreachable`: the next page resumes, skipping what is fresh), or when `navigator.storage.estimate()` passes 50 MB (`storage budget reached`, read every 10 fetches; unchecked on iOS 16, where the caps bound it). `warmed-at` is written, under the lock and only in the run's generation, when it ran to the end or hit the budget.
7. One line: `[sw] warmed 31 pages, 12 images in 8.2 s (78 fresh, 2 removed, 0 refused, usage 6.1 MB of 12 GB)`, or `[sw] warm stopped, <why>: warmed …`. A WARM_PAGES while a run is under way runs once more after it (the next account's first page after a drop).

**Caps and storage.** `pages-v2` holds 400 entries, `images-v2` 600 with no age limit (photo URLs are immutable, and an age limit only forced a refetch, a statement each). The demo persona's warmed origin measured 4.4 MB in Chromium (2026-10-02; budget 10 MB). `pwa.js` calls `navigator.storage.persist()` once per launch in a standalone signed-in app and logs the answer: WebKit grants it to a Home Screen app by heuristics, which keeps the warmed caches out of eviction. In a Safari tab ITP's 7-day rule can still empty them: offline reading is dependable in the installed app only.

**Invalidation.** Online, every tap is NetworkFirst and replaces the copy, so a warm never makes a page shown online older. A copy read offline is at most about a day old and says so (freshness). A write made online leaves that garment's warmed full page stale until the next visit or warm.

**HTML caching.** `send()` (`src/web/render.ts`) gives every HTML answer `Cache-Control: private, no-cache` unless its route set one (the token page's `no-store`): no shared cache may store an account's page and the browser revalidates its copy (no-cache keeps the back-forward cache). `/file/**` and static answers are unchanged (`test/integration/delivery.spec.ts`).

**Specs.** `test/integration/offline-warm.spec.ts` (the list, the grantee, the caps, the `(warm)` log); `test/offline-warm.spec.ts` (the demo wardrobe offline after one warm, every page with its age and photos, the grid scrolled, under 10 MB; a second open warms nothing, a day later only the stale copy, a deleted garment's page removed); `test/stale-pages.spec.ts`, "a warmed wardrobe and the session" (another account's sign-in empties both caches; a warm under way when the account changes stores nothing more). Specs whose caches must hold their own visits alone call `withoutWarming(context)` (`test/support/service-worker.ts`: `saveData`, which `pwa.js` honours).
