# Insights

`src/wardrobe/insights.ts` (pure), `src/web/insights/`, `src/web/mcp/tools/insights.ts` (#17, plan section 10). How the closet is actually used, computed on every request from the wear log; nothing stored. **The owner's own, like wears**: `GET /wardrobe/insights[?unworn=30|90|180]` (in the Wardrobe's ⋯ menu beside Plans and the shopping list; under `/wardrobe`, so the dock marks Wardrobe) ignores `?ownerId=`, and a share never reaches it (matrix row in `authorization-outfits.spec.ts`). `?unworn=` is navigation state: anything else is 90, never a 400.

- **Two statements** (`queries.ts`, run together by `readInsights`, which the page and `wardrobe_stats` share): `insightGarments`, one row per garment in the closet (`inCloset`) with its wear days ever, in the last year, the last day worn, and days since it and since `acquired_on` (Postgres `date - date` against the household's today, so the pure side does no date arithmetic); and `wornPairs`, the self-join of one year's distinct (garment, day) pairs, at most 5. `wardrobeInsights` computes everything else from those rows. The page is the owner row plus these two (`recordQueries` in `insights.spec.ts` and `insights-seed.spec.ts`): **a new figure is computed from the rows in `insights.ts`, not a new query**, unless it needs data the rows lack.
- **Definitions** (`insights.ts` states them; the specs pin each on a known closet): the closet is `inCloset` garments, each counted once whatever its quantity (worn when any copy was); wears are distinct days. _Worn in the last N days_ (30, 90, 365): a wear today or in the N - 1 days before. _Unworn in N days_ (30, 90, 180): never worn or last worn N days ago or more, among garments owned at least N days (no `acquired_on` counts as owned long enough), never worn first, then the longest since; each with "Style this" (`styleThisUrl`, `urls.ts`: Styling's `/styling?with=` (#42), as on the garment page). _Most worn_: by wear days ever, ties the most recent first. _Least worn_: among garments owned `LEAST_WORN_MIN_OWNED_DAYS` (90) or more, leaving out the most worn. _Cost per wear_: `price` is per piece and, after "Bought it", the price paid (it overwrites the listed one), so the cost is price × quantity, divided by wear days, in cents rounded to the cent; no price is left out (counted), 0 wears is "not worn yet" and never divided; best is the cheaper half at most (up to 5), worst the dearer half, so no garment is in both. _Pairs_: two closet garments worn on the same day (whichever entries held them) on at least 2 days of the last year. _Colours_: each colour's share of the closet's garments and of the last year's wear days, a garment in k colours counting 1/k to each (strips of `.ms-swatch--<colour>` segments grown by `flex-grow`). _Categories and brands_: garments, pieces (quantity), share of the closet and of the year's wear days; brands grouped regardless of case and spaces under the first-added garment's spelling, 8 named and the rest summed. _Condition_: needs_repair and replace_soon counts, linking to the grid's "Needs attention" filter (`?attention=true`).
- **Seed**: Theo's 13 weeks give every figure a real answer (`insights-seed.spec.ts`, the clock pinned to the anchor); Dana's untagged closet has no wears (the "nothing worn yet" note); Riley's is the empty state.
- **Logs**: the route logs one debug line per read (`Insights for user 3: 80 garment(s), 5 pair(s), unworn 90 days in 12 ms`); the request line is the root hook's.
