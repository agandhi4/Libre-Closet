# Wears and washes

`src/web/wears/` and `src/wardrobe/availability.ts` (#7, plan section 1). **The owner's own records, like outfits and the calendar**: wears, washes and away are never shown to or changed by a grantee (the garment routes answer a grantee 403 with `?ownerId=`, 404 without; the grid computes no wash state for a share; `/laundry` ignores `?ownerId=`). A grantee sees what are garment properties: `quantity` and `condition` (which a MANAGE grantee may set).

- **`garment_wear`** (`drizzle/0011_wears_washes.sql`): one row per (calendar entry, garment) when the entry is marked worn, or one per (garment, day) for "Wore today" (`outfit_calendar_id` null; `garment_wear_garment_id_day_single_unique` keeps it to one a day). **Counts are distinct days**, never rows: two entries on one day are one wear. Deleting a garment or its owner takes its wears; archiving keeps them.
- **Deleting an entry and deleting an outfit differ on purpose.** Deleting a calendar entry (`deleteEntry`, the chip's ×) removes exactly its wears, like unmarking: the user says the entry was wrong. Deleting an outfit keeps the days it was worn: `deleteOutfit` calls `detachOutfitWears` in its transaction (the outfit and its entries locked) before the delete, which turns each entry wear into a day-level wear (`outfit_calendar_id` null; where the garment already has one that day, that one stays). Without it `outfit_calendar`'s cascade would take the entries and `garment_wear`'s cascade their wears: history erased by tidying the outfit list. The same rule holds for the entries' outfit selfies (#19): deleting the entry deletes its selfie (row, then bytes after commit); deleting the outfit keeps each one as a look on its day (`selfie.outfit_calendar_id` goes null by its foreign key; see Outfit selfies). "Wore today"'s undo deletes only today's day-level wear, so a kept wear from another day cannot be undone; one kept today reads as "Wore today" and its undo is today's to make.
- **One writer each** (`src/web/wears/queries.ts`): `setEntryWorn` (the calendar pill and the seed; the entry locked, one transaction; its change, `changeEntryWorn`, is also `wearOutfitOn`'s, on the entry its planning statement locked, #166. Marking snapshots the outfit's garments of the owner as they are now, so **editing an outfit never changes past wears**; unmarking deletes exactly that entry's rows; an explicit `worn`, absent from pills cached before it, toggles; a day after `APP_TIMEZONE`'s today is refused, 409, and the calendar shows no pill there), `setWoreToday` (and its same-day undo), `markWashed` (`last_washed_on` = today; the garment page's Washed, `/laundry`'s batch, the seed's Sundays), `setAway`. `insertEntry` creates entries unworn: nothing else sets `worn_at`.
- **The rules** (`src/wardrobe/availability.ts`, pure, `availability.spec.ts`): wears since the wash are days after `last_washed_on` (a wear on the wash day counts before it); the limit is `wash_after_wears` or the role's default (top and one-piece 1, bottom 3, layer 10, footwear, accessory, bag and none never; `NEVER_WASH` (0) is an explicit never, as null is "the default"); **dirty copies = floor(wears / limit), capped at the quantity**. The plan wrote ceil, which marks jeans (limit 3) dirty after one wear against its own threshold rule; floor is that rule per copy and equals ceil at limit 1. "Needs a wash" (laundry, filter, badges) is a dirty copy; **available** is in the closet (`inCloset`: not archived, not a wishlist item), not away and a clean copy left.
- **The generator-facing predicate**: `availableGarment()` (SQL condition, `src/web/wears/queries.ts`) and `isAvailable()` (pure, `availability.ts`) are the same rule; use one of them, never a third. `wears.spec.ts` checks the SQL forms (`dirtyCopiesSql`, `availableGarment`) against the pure ones over a case table. **Condition never counts**: `needs_repair` is still in the closet and worn; `away: repair` is physically at the shop.
- **Routes** (wears plugin): `POST /wardrobe/:id/wear` (`worn=1|0`), `/washed`, `/away` (`away` '' | lent | repair, `awayNote`): to htmx, wear and washed answer `#garment-wear-status` (the wear line: cost, Style this, Wore today, Washed; "Where it is" is its own section, `#garment-away`, so the away form is never swapped) and away, an `AutosaveForm`, its status line; a plain post 303 to the garment. Each writer refuses what it may not change (another's garment, a wishlist item), so the three look nothing up before writing, and only a write that changed nothing reads the garment to answer 404 or 409 (#160, `src/web/wardrobe/garment-page.md`); `GET /laundry` (what needs a wash, checked, then what was worn but is not due, unchecked; archived and away left out; one `PostForm`) and `POST /laundry` (`ids`, others' ignored; 303 `?washed=N` for the toast).
- **Garment form**: quantity (1-30), "Wash after" and condition + note, guarded by their own marker `care=1` (Gotchas). "Wash after" is the owner's record: `formAudience` (`garment-form.tsx`: one's own wardrobe, or a clone, which lands there) decides both whether the form renders it and whether the save reads it (`readGarmentForm(body, audience)`, `validation.ts`), so a MANAGE grantee's edit or new garment never shows or sets it (a posted one is left as stored), and a clone of a shared garment starts at the role's default (`storedFormValues(stored, { owner })`). A new owner-only form field joins `FormAudience`. The note is kept only with a problem (a check constraint agrees); `away_note` only with `away`.
- **Offline**: these writes and the calendar pill carry `data-needs-network`; while offline or reconnecting `connectivity.js` (`guardWrites`, on every state change and swap) sets the real `disabled` and `aria-disabled` on their controls, marked `data-offline-disabled` so reconnecting re-enables only those and never a control disabled for its own reason, and `main.css` dims them through `data-connectivity` on `<html>` (the banner says why). Any new write can opt in the same way (`test/wears.spec.ts` covers keyboard and restore). A page that needs to say what offline means for it adds an element with `data-offline-note`, hidden online by `main.css` and shown offline or reconnecting (the gallery's "picking needs a connection").
- **Backfill**: `0011` inserted the wears of every entry already marked worn from its outfit's slots at migration time (the only record), owner-checked, dated the entry's day (`wears-migration.spec.ts`).

## Gotchas

- **Wears count days, not entries, and every new wear count must too.** With several outfits a day, one garment has a `garment_wear` row per worn entry that held it (so unmarking one entry removes exactly its rows); every count and wash rule is `count(distinct day)` (`wearsSinceWashSql`, `wearSummary`, `availability.ts`, insights' `insightGarments` and `wornPairs`). A new query that counts rows would count "office then dinner, same shoes" twice (`wears.spec.ts` has that case).
- **Condition is not availability, and `away: repair` is not `needs_repair`.** Condition (good, needs_repair, replace_soon) is a garment property anyone who can edit sets; it never feeds `availableGarment`/`isAvailable`. Away (lent, repair) is the owner's record that the garment is physically elsewhere, and does. Do not merge the two.
