# Trips

`src/wardrobe/packing.ts` (pure) and `src/web/trips/` (#10; plan section 4, the redesign's "Calendar › Trips"). A trip is **a standalone list of outfits** for some days away (owner decision: not a date range over the calendar) and the packing list derived from them. **The owner's own, like outfits**: no route takes `?ownerId=`, another user's trip, trip outfit or extra is a 404 with nothing written, shares never reach it (matrix rows in `authorization-trips.spec.ts`, a gallery pick for someone else's trip included).

- **Tables** (`drizzle/0024_trips.sql`): `trip` (owner, name, `destination` text, `latitude`/`longitude` rounded like the weather home and only with a destination (`trip_location_check`), `starts_on`/`ends_on` dates, at most `MAX_TRIP_DAYS` (60) by the form, notes); `trip_outfit` (trip, outfit, optional `day` and `occasion`, OCCASIONS by a check: the same outfit once a day, or once without a day, by two partial unique indexes, so adding again is `on conflict do nothing` and keeps the first occasion, the calendar's rule); `trip_item` (the extras: label once per trip in any case, `packed`); `trip_garment_packed` (trip, garment: a row means packed). All cascade with the trip; `trip_outfit` with its outfit, the mark with its garment.
- **The packing list is derived, never stored** (`packingList`, pure, `packing.spec.ts`; read by `tripModel`, `model.ts`, five statements): the distinct garments of the trip's outfits, grouped by role top to toe. **Copies needed**: a garment is worn once a day whatever the occasions (Day 2's daytime and dinner jeans are one wear), an outfit without a day is a wear of its own (`tripWears`); nothing is washed on the trip, so with a wash limit k the trip needs `ceil(wears / k)` copies, one for what is never washed (`copiesNeeded`); what can be packed is that up to the quantity owned. **Warnings**, only while the trip is ahead or on (a finished trip's list is a record): `too-few` (the trip needs more than owned), `wash` (up to departure: fewer clean copies now than to pack, from `availability.ts`'s rules and `wearsSinceWashSql`), `away`, `archived`.
- **Packed marks survive edits and leave with their garment.** Keyed by garment, so renaming the trip, adding an outfit or re-saving one that keeps the garment changes nothing. Every writer that can take a garment off a trip's list prunes the marks it orphans in its own transaction (`prunePacked`, `packed.ts`): `removeTripOutfit`, `updateOutfit` and `deleteOutfit` (`src/web/outfits/queries.ts`, through `tripsOfOutfit`), and `setPacked` itself; a garment that comes back comes back unpacked. The reads join through the list, so a mark left by a race (an outfit edit beside a packing tap) never shows and goes at the trip's next write.
- **One writer each** (`queries.ts`, every write under the trip's row lock, `lockTrip`, taken before any other): `createTrip`, `updateTrip` (a new destination name clears the location it no longer describes; outfits on days the new dates leave out move to "Any day", one row each, so `day` is always one of the trip's days), `setTripDestination`, `deleteTrip`, `addTripOutfit` (a day outside the trip is refused), `pickForTrip` (the gallery's pick and `plan_trip_outfit` with garments: `pickIdea` then the trip, one transaction, trip lock before `lockOwner`), `removeTripOutfit`, `addTripItems`, `copyTripItems` (another trip's labels, unpacked, those already there skipped, so twice adds nothing), `setItemsPacked`, `removeTripItem`; `setPacked` (`packed.ts`: only garments on the list are marked). The form reads through `readTripForm` (`validation.ts`), which the seed posts through too.
- **"Wearing this today"** (`POST /trips/:id/outfits/:tripOutfitId/wear`, while the trip is on, else 409): the calendar's writers, `wearOutfitOn` (`src/web/calendar/queries.ts`: `insertEntry` for today and the trip outfit's occasion, all day without one, then `setEntryWorn`, one transaction, idempotent), which Today's "Wear this" (`wearIdea`) uses too. The trip page says "Worn today" for an outfit with a worn entry today.
- **The destination's forecast** (`forecast.ts`, `tripForecast`): the weather's own service and cache (`forecastFor`) at the trip's rounded location, for its days the 16-day forecast reaches, each with what it asks of an all-day outfit (the person's offset); later days say from when their forecast arrives and, meanwhile, show the destination's **typical weather** ("Typically 50–65 °F, 24% rain chance", under a note that it is the last 10 years' average and not a forecast; `data-typical-day`), from its climate normals (`normalsFor`, see Weather), each with what a typical day asks and its own Ideas link; nothing is fetched for a trip without a located destination or one that is over, and one that starts past the forecast fetches only the normals. A forecast Open-Meteo has not answered for the place marks the near days `unavailable` ("No forecast for the destination right now", `forecastUnavailable` in `get_trip`) and keeps the far days' typical weather (`forecast.spec.tsx`). Located through the weather's geocoding search on the trip page (`/trips/:id/places`, `WEATHER_SEARCH_LIMIT`, `secretPath`), set by `POST /trips/:id/destination` (`WEATHER_LOCATION_LIMIT`). **Weather-aware ideas**: the gallery with `for=trip:ID[:day]` passes the destination as `IdeasInput.place` (`ideasFor`), so a trip day's ideas match its weather (none, never home's, while the destination is not located); a day past the forecast is matched to the destination's typical day (`typicalDay`, the same `weatherNeeds` and `assessOutfit`), and the page says so ("Typically feels 52–65 °F (not a forecast)", `data-ideas-weather="typical"`, "Fits the typical weather"). Pages never render the forecast themselves: `GET /trips/:id/weather` is a fragment the page loads.
- **Routes** (`routes.tsx`): `GET /trips` (the Calendar's Trips tab, `CalendarTabs`; `sections.ts` marks `/trips` as Calendar), `GET /trips/new`, `POST /trips` (303 `?created=1`), `GET /trips/:id` (days with their outfits in occasion order and "Any day", each with Ideas and "+ Add"; the packing list and the extras as `AutosaveForm`s posting the checked and `shown` ids, answering the status line and the summary out of band; notes), `GET /trips/:id/edit`, `POST /trips/:id`, `DELETE /trips/:id` (`navigateTo` the list; the outfits stay), `GET /trips/:id/outfits/new?day=&occasion=` (the calendar plan page's shape: day and occasion chips, Ideas, the saved outfits as `SavedOutfitButton`s), `POST /trips/:id/outfits`, `POST /trips/:id/outfits/:tripOutfitId/delete` and `/wear`, `POST /trips/:id/packed`, `POST /trips/:id/items`, `/items/packed`, `/items/:itemId/delete`, `/items/copy`; with `WEATHER_ENABLED` only, `GET /trips/:id/weather`, `GET /trips/:id/places?q=` and `POST /trips/:id/destination`. Network first like every page but the tab roots; offline the worker's copy shows with its writes disabled (`data-needs-network`, a `data-offline-note`).
- **Logs** (context `Web`): the trip's create, update (outfits moved to any day, location cleared), delete; `Outfit <id> added to|already on trip <id> by user <id> (<day>, <occasion>)`, taken off (with the marks removed), worn (`entry <id> planned|already planned, marked worn (<n> wears logged)|already worn`); packing and extras set (`N packed, M unpacked`), copied; the destination located (never the place); the page's model at debug.
- **Seed**: Theo's Austin conference (the Events' 19 to 21 August, moved with them), the bible's Trips tables: five outfits over three days (the travel outfit both flying days, a run, the office and a rooftop evening on day 2), five extras (three packed), nine garments packed of fifteen; located with weather on.

## Gotchas

- **A writer that can take a garment off a trip's packing list prunes its packed marks** (`prunePacked`, `src/web/trips/packed.ts`, in the same transaction). The list is derived from the trip's outfits' slots, so today that is removing a trip outfit, editing an outfit's slots and deleting an outfit (`tripsOfOutfit` first: `trip_outfit` cascades). Styling's save (#42) edits through `updateOutfit` and creates through `pickIdea`, so it inherits it; a new path that changes slots or trip outfits (a bulk outfit delete) must call it too, or a garment that comes back to the list comes back already packed.
