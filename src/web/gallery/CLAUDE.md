# Outfit gallery

`src/wardrobe/generator.ts` (pure) and `src/web/gallery/`. The model: **the gallery chooses whole outfits** (the Outfits page's Ideas and Saved tabs); composing row by row is Styling; **the generator is the one source of suggestions**, reached only through `ideasFor` (`ideas.ts`): the Ideas tab, `suggest_outfits`, Today (today's all-day ideas, 3 a page, through `todayIdeas`) and Styling's Shuffle (its locked rows as `IdeasInput.locked`, read by `styledGarmentsSql` in the same statement through `ideasColumns` and `ideasFrom`); and its siblings `goesWithCloset` (see Wishlist: the whole closet with a wishlist item locked) and `browseIdea` (Shuffle over a shared wardrobe, see Styling). A pick goes where its destination says through `pickTo` (`pick.ts`, shared with Styling's Save) and a `?for=` is aimed through `aimIdeas` (`aim.ts`, shared with Shuffle). Private like outfits: the signed-in user's garments, capsules and outfits; `?ownerId=` is not read. Tab labels: `gallery.TAB_*`; Ideas lives inside the Outfits page (`outfit-tabs.tsx`: static without a destination, so `/outfits` stays byte-stable for the service worker; a day's `?for=` travels between the tabs).

- **The generator** (`generateIdeas`, generic over the caller's garment type; `generator.spec.ts`): templates top + bottom + footwear or one-piece + footwear (footwear only when the closet has any), a layer only when the weather asks (the best-scoring option, then no layer, then the most rested). **The pool is the caller's**: `ideaPoolSql` reads `availableGarment()` (in the closet, not away, a clean copy left) and `inCapsule`; the generator never widens it, draws only layer, one-piece, top, bottom and footwear, and lets locked garments (any role, clean or not) ride along. Rotation: each role is drawn weighted by days since its last wear (`1 + min(idle, 60) / 10`; never worn is the cap). **Hard rules** (never relaxed): at most two colours beyond the neutrals (`neutralFor(role, color)`, the one place: black, white, grey, beige and brown on anything, and **blue on bottoms** (owner's rule: jeans and navy chinos go with anything; a blue top or layer counts); a colour counts once however many garments carry it) and one patterned garment (anything but solid, or the colour `pattern`); no pair in `generator_avoid`; no combination equal to a saved outfit, compared on the drawn roles (a saved outfit with a belt still counts); every locked garment in every idea. **Soft rules, a score**: `assessOutfit` against the day's `weatherNeeds` (the occasion's window, the personal offset) plus one per formality step outside `OCCASION_HINTS`' range; ideas scoring 0 come first in draw order, then the near misses best first, each with its problems named (`IdeaProblem`).
- **Seeded and paged, never materialized**: the draws are `seededRandom('generator', GENERATOR_VERSION, seed)`; a page asks for `offset + limit + 1` fitting ideas and stops drawing once it has them, else spends all `MAX_DRAWS` (2000) and appends the sorted near misses. **It stops sooner only where the page cannot change**: once `want` ideas score the floor (`scoreFloor`: the locks' formality misses plus each open role's smallest, since misses only add up and the weather's part is never negative; 0 when a fit is possible); no later draw scores lower, and a tie ranks after. It also stops when the locks break a colour, pattern or clash rule (no idea: those rules only worsen as garments are added, `HardRules.compatible`) and once every distinct base has been drawn (`baseCount`). A gallery page past what fits still spends 2000, since the weather part has no floor. Within a draw, a base breaking a hard rule on its own is never scored, layers are tried in preference order and the first scoring 0 wins unscored rivals (`assessOutfit` is most of a draw's cost), and rotation weights are computed once a request. `generator-output.spec.ts` pins the exact output (ideas, order, scores, `more`) of seeded closets: a speed-up that changes it is not one. The fitting list is prefix-stable: page 1 never changes when page 3 is read; the same (pool, seed, page) is the same page. A base is judged deterministically (layer included): one idea however often drawn. The day's seed (`dailySeed(today)`) opens the gallery; Shuffle is `shuffledSeed(seed)` (no `Math.random` or `Date.now` anywhere in the data path).
- **Routes** (`routes.tsx`, own plugin): `GET /outfits/ideas[?for=day:D&occasion=O][&capsule=][&with=][&seed=][&page=]` (the page), `GET /outfits/ideas/more?...&page=N` (next cards and sentinel, a fragment), `POST /outfits/ideas/pick` (native `PostForm`: `garmentId` × n plus the destination; 303 to the week for a day, else to the new outfit; with `replace` the idea takes that entry's place through `replaceEntryOutfit`, and `replace` without a day is a 400), `POST /outfits/ideas/feedback` (`nudgeTemperatureOffset`; 404 without `WEATHER_ENABLED`; 303 back), `POST /outfits/ideas/avoid` ("Clashes": `garmentId` × 2; htmx answers 200 empty and the card is swapped away with `hx-swap="delete"`, a plain post 303 back), `POST /outfits/ideas/allow` (the garment page's undo; 303 to the first garment). `?for=`/`?occasion=` are navigation state through `parseDestination` (none: today, all day, and a pick just saves), `?seed=`/`?page=` fall back to the day's seed and page 1 (past `MAX_IDEAS_PAGE`, 50, too); the paging surfaces (the strip, `suggest_outfits`) read through `ideasPage`, which answers `more: false` on that page, so the sentinel never asks for page 51; `?capsule=`/`?with=` name data: not an id 400, not the user's own capsule or closet garment 404. A write's `for` is data: a malformed day is a 400 (it must read back as posted, `destinationTarget`). `for=trip:ID[:day]` names the owner's trip (else a 404; a day not among its days is dropped): its ideas use the destination's forecast and its pick is `pickForTrip`, 303 to the trip (`?picked=1`).
- **A pick is one transaction, and happens once**: `pickIdea` (`src/web/outfits/pick.ts`, a Queryable; the outfit write is Outfits', so `ideas.ts` stays read-only and imports neither `ownerTransaction` nor the calendar) runs in `ownerTransaction` (Calendar's owner lock), checks every garment is the owner's and in the closet (else refused whole, nothing written, the garments named by the route: `garmentsGoneError`, a 409 for an archived or wishlist one, a 404 for one not theirs; see Outfits) and **keeps them locked `FOR SHARE`, in id order, until the outfit is saved** (`pickedGarments`): an archive or delete waits for the pick, or the pick waits and leaves the garment out, so no pick saves an archived garment or an emptied slot; in the same statement it looks for an outfit of the owner's with exactly these garments (`sameGarmentsOutfit` from `src/web/outfits/queries.ts`, empty slots aside). **One exists** (a double tap, a retried `pick_outfit`): it is the answer, `alreadySaved`, planned on the destination's day through `insertEntry`, and nothing is created; a person's pick (not the planner's `plannedBy: 'auto'`) of an outfit "Plan my week" created **takes it over** (`reuseOutfit`'s `adoptPlannerOutfit`, `src/web/week-plan/adopt.ts`: its `week_plan_entry.outfit_created` rows cleared in the pick's transaction), so "Already saved" stays true through Undo and the re-plan (`PickResult.adopted`, logged "taken over from the week planner"); the redirect carries `?alreadySaved=1`, which the outfit page and the week show as "Already saved" (`AlreadySavedToast`, stripped from the address). **None**: the slots top to toe (`OUTFIT_ORDER` by role), the name (`ideaName`: the garments' names or categories, top to toe, within `OUTFIT_NAME_MAX`) and `createOutfit`, which joins the pick's owner transaction and writes the outfit (checking once more, in its insert, for one of these garments, see Outfits), its slots and the calendar entry. `gallery.spec.ts` fails the entry's insert (no outfit or slot left), picks twice, and holds one pick open while a second waits (one outfit, one entry).
- **`generator_avoid`** (`drizzle/0018_generator_avoid.sql`): `(owner_id, garment_a_id, garment_b_id)` primary key, `garment_a_id < garment_b_id` by a check, both garments and the owner cascade. One writer, `avoidPair` (both garments the owner's, owned now or once, locked `FOR SHARE`; `on conflict do nothing`: 'added', 'already' or 'not-found'), and `allowPair`, its undo. The garment page lists its pairs ("Never paired with", `avoided.tsx`) with "Allow again", owner only.
- **The page** (`ideas-page.tsx`): a `PageMain wide` page whose cards sit in the shared `SnapStrip` (`size="page"`: the strip's width on a phone, 18rem cards centred from `lg`, `listbox={false}` as the cards hold forms; `#idea-strip`; the page renders `SnapStripsInit`): swiping is the browser's scrolling, no library or touch handlers, and a mouse steps with the strip's buttons (#321). Today's rows and Goes-with use the same strip (`peek`; `pair`, or `page` for one). The last child is the sentinel (`hx-trigger="intersect once"`, `hx-swap="outerHTML"`; Gotchas). A card is the shared `OutfitCollage` (`src/web/outfits/collage.tsx`; sizes `card` (Ideas, Today, the outfit page), `tile` (the Saved grid) and `thumb` (the garment page, the calendar)), the name, its reasons, the pick and "Not this" (too warm / too cold, "Not today" which only removes the card, the clash pairs). The header: day and occasion (back to the plan page), capsule menu, "With …" for `?with=`, weather, Shuffle. An unnamed garment is its category's label wherever named (`name ?? categoryLabel(category)`). The forecast is read only for days it covers (today to 15 days ahead); past it only a trip day has weather, its destination's typical day (see Trips). **Offline**: the cards the worker cached show; every write is `data-needs-network`; a `data-offline-note` explains.
- **Entry points**: the calendar's plan page ("Choose from ideas" with its `?for=`), a capsule's "Swipe outfits" (`?capsule=`, the owner's), the Saved tab. "Style this" opens Styling; the gallery's `?with=` stays for its links and `suggest_outfits`.
- **What a request reads, and why** (`gallery.spec.ts`, `today-home.spec.ts` and `weather.spec.ts` pin the counts). **`ideasFor` is one statement** (`selectScalars`), each part a scalar subquery owned by its module, as `gridContext` does: the pool (`ideaPoolSql`, read back by `readPool`), saved outfits and clashes (`generatorMemorySql`), the person's weather settings with their location's cached forecast row (`weatherWithForecastSql`; only with `WEATHER_ENABLED`, a day from today on, and not when the caller read it already, Today's `ownWeather`), and, for the page, the capsule menu (`capsuleNamesSql`; `IdeasInput.capsuleMenu`). The forecast is fetched again only on a miss or a trip's other place. So the page is 2 statements (the session, the ideas) and `/more` 2; `?capsule=` (`findCapsule`), `?with=` (`styledGarment`) and a trip (`findTrip`) each add one before it, since the route validates them first; reading them inside would change `ideasFor`'s input, which Today and MCP share (Styling reads its checks beside `ideasColumns` and answers with `ideasFrom`, `ideasFor`'s two halves). A pick: the owner lock's transaction and one statement (`pickedGarments`: the garments `FOR SHARE OF garment` joined to the outfit they already are), then `createOutfit` (whose slots check their garments inside the insert) or the planner take-over. A clash is one statement (`avoidPair`: a CTE locks both garments and inserts only when it found both).
- **Logs** (context `Web`): `Idea picked by user <id>: outfit <id> of garments <ids>, planned <day> (<occasion>)|saved`, `Garments <a> and <b> avoided|already avoided together by user <id>`, the allow and the temperature feedback; the page's generation at debug (count, page, seed, day, occasion, scope, weather, ms).

## Gotchas

- **Suggestions come from the generator, through `ideasFor`, and its randomness is seeded.** A new surface that suggests outfits calls `ideasFor` (or `generateIdeas` with a pool from `ideaPoolSql`; "Goes with my closet" is one other pool, `closetGarmentsSql`, with its item only ever `locked`, and the week planner the other, `weekPoolSql`: dirty garments included, judged per day by `cleanCopies` with the week's own wears), never a query of its own, or the availability, colour, clash and duplicate rules fork. Nothing in its data path may use `Math.random` or the clock: a gallery page is a function of (pool as a set, seed, page), and the sentinel's page 2 must continue page 1. Postgres never promises row order without ORDER BY, so `generateIdeas` orders each role by id before it draws by position; a new seeded path that draws by position does the same. Bump `GENERATOR_VERSION` to reshuffle every seed on purpose.
- **A sentinel inside a scroll container triggers on `intersect`, and a delete swap needs a 200.** htmx's `revealed` listens to the window's scroll, so a sentinel at the end of a horizontal strip never fires with it; `intersect` uses an IntersectionObserver, which sees the container's clipping. htmx swaps nothing on a 204, so a write whose answer removes its element (`hx-swap="delete"`, "Clashes") answers 200 with an empty body.
