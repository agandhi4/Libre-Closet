# Muse's outfits

Part of Outfits (`CLAUDE.md` beside this file).

(#335, `proposals.ts`, `muse-cards.tsx`): an ordinary outfit the agent proposed carries `proposed_at` (the mark), its token, `proposal_note` and the owner's `reaction` (the plan looks' machine reused, `src/wardrobe/look-reaction.ts`), a reason when declined (`OUTFIT_DISMISS_REASONS`), and `reacted_at`. `drizzle/0041` made the drafted plans' looks these, linked by `plan_look_id` until `0045_drop-plans` dropped the plans (#337). **Which list it is on is derived, never stored**: the owner's (`ownersOutfit`, `references.ts`: never proposed, or loved while complete, as "Save" is Love on a complete outfit) is the Saved grid, every picker (`savedOutfitsSql`, the calendar's plan page included) and the garment page's strip; the rest are the tab's **From Muse** section above the grid (waiting: proposed, sent back, or loved with pieces to buy) and its collapsed "N set aside" (declined, Undo). **One writer**, `reactToOutfit` (`reactions.ts`; owner lock, the outfit `FOR UPDATE`, the machine, one update), through `POST /outfits/:id/love` (Save when complete), `/dismiss` (a reason, else 400; an optional note) and `/undo`, each a native `PostForm` answering 303 to `returnTo` with a toast (`?decided=`); 404 for another's or a non-proposal, 409 for a stale move. A card has **one primary**: Save (complete), Love (pieces to buy), Choose the pieces (loved, to buy: the first piece's page), Edit in Styling (a pick set aside: "Needs a replacement"); its pieces open the photo viewer, a piece to buy wears its price and "Unlocks N" (the inbox's count, `museUnlocksInputsSql`, in the tab's one statement). Never read while picking for a day. **What may be held is one rule, `outfitMayBeHeld`** (`references.ts`: complete, and the owner's): every hold writer (`upsertEntry`, `planToWear`, `setEntryOutfit`, `addTripOutfit`) writes only where it holds and refuses with `refuseUnholdable` (`OutfitIncomplete`, or `OutfitNotYours` for a proposal not saved); the outfit page offers Plan only then. A person's save or pick of a pending proposal's exact garments adopts it (`reuseOutfit` → `adoptProposal`, the writer's Love), so a complete one may then be planned. No reaction moves an outfit that is the owner's already (`'owners'`, a 409). `list_outfits` lists the owner's own; `get_outfit` carries `proposal`. **The agent proposes through `proposeOutfit`** (`queries.ts`, #337's `suggest_outfit`): createOutfit's insert once per garment set, marked (`proposed_at`, token, note, `proposed`), never planned; garments already the owner's outfit, or a proposal declined, are refused, a proposal not declined is the answer (`alreadyProposed`); it never adopts. A proposal not loved is never deleted (`deleteOutfit` answers `'a-proposal'`, a 409; the ⋯ menu hides Delete). The Saved tab is 2 statements with the session's (`outfit-statements.spec.ts`), static markup so bare `/outfits` stays byte-stable. `muse-outfits.spec.ts`, `muse-outfits-migration.spec.ts`.
